The Pulse Shift: Kory White Featured in Annapolis Tech News Velocity Series

PULSE RevOps founder Kory White sits down with Margaux Kent of Annapolis Tech News for a wide-ranging conversation on architecture, agility, and the 72-hour AI-driven build of a complete revenue operating system.

📅 April 14, 2026 📍 Stevensville, MD 📰 Annapolis Tech News ⏱ 14 min read

STEVENSVILLE, MD — April 14, 2026 — PULSE RevOps founder Kory White is the inaugural subject of The Velocity Series, a new long-form interview franchise launched this month by Annapolis Tech News, the independent publication covering startups, founders, and innovation in Maryland's capital.

In the interview — conducted by ATN senior editor Margaux Kent on the island in Stevensville — White walks through the 72-hour generative-AI build of PULSE, the free revenue operating system now downloaded by thousands of sales leaders, founders, and RevOps practitioners. The piece is the first in a series ATN plans to publish monthly, profiling the operators behind the Chesapeake region's fastest-moving tech companies.

Key Themes From the Interview

The conversation centers on White's concept of "Performance Synchronization" — the idea that most revenue organizations are not failing from a lack of leads, but from a lack of alignment between marketing, sales, and field execution. White uses the phrase "Red Box zones" to describe the pipeline friction points where activity is high but conversion is dead, and describes PULSE as a public utility for finding and fixing them.

"Activity is a vanity metric. Synchronization is the only metric that matters." — Kory White, in Annapolis Tech News, April 2026

White also discusses the influence of former McDonald's and MGM Chief Digital Officer Atif Rafiq, whose "Decision Sprint" philosophy inspired PULSE's build cadence. "Atif is the master of speed of learning," White tells Kent. "He argues that agility isn't about moving fast; it's about knowing more today than you did yesterday. That's exactly how I built PULSE."

The Human Element

Despite PULSE's heavy automation layer — AI coaching, auto-forecasting, the Fixator autonomous-mode engine — White is emphatic in the interview that AI doesn't close deals. "AI doesn't close deals; it just clears the path so a human can," he tells Kent. "A great salesperson today needs to be like a pro athlete — high discipline, high tech, but with a soul."

"If you use AI to spam people, you lose. If you use AI to understand your customer's Red Box before you even pick up the phone, you win." — Kory White

What's Next

White confirms in the interview that he is actively exploring Chief Revenue Officer opportunities with high-growth companies preparing for their next stage of scale. "I'm not just looking for a job," he tells Kent. "I'm looking for a mission."

The full interview — along with bonus media, a video walkthrough of the 72-hour PULSE build, and the Revenue Alignment Audit checklist — is available at annapolistechnews.com.
