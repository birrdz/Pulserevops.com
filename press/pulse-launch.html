Annapolis Executive Kory J. White Unveils Free 'Pulse' Revenue Framework

Former Verizon Cellular Sales Regional President releases proprietary Revenue Operations ecosystem free to small and medium businesses — a passion project born from $3B in enterprise-scale experience.
📅 September 12, 2025 📍 Annapolis, MD ✍️ Kory White 🏷 Launch · Revenue Operations · SMB

ANNAPOLIS, MD — September 12, 2025 — Kory J. White, a high-impact executive leader with a 22-year background at Verizon Cellular Sales, has officially announced the launch of PULSE, a proprietary Revenue Operations (RevOps) ecosystem designed to revolutionize how modern organizations align sales, marketing, and customer success into a single revenue engine.

While White is currently exploring senior leadership opportunities — specifically Chief Revenue Officer and Executive Vice President roles at high-growth organizations — he has made the strategic decision to release his proprietary PULSE framework as a totally free resource. Available at pulserevops.com, the platform is specifically engineered to help small and medium-sized businesses align their sales teams and operational workflows without enterprise-level budgets or software costs.

"After 22 years in the industry, I know the 'Red Box' zones — the places where revenue is lost due to operational misalignment — affect the shoestring-budget entrepreneur just as much as the $3B enterprise. PULSE is my way of giving those owners a fighting chance." — Kory J. White, Chief Revenue Officer

A Passion Project Born from $3B Experience

Over two decades, White architected "Greenfield" territories into $200M+ ARR revenue engines and served as a key operational leader on the team that scaled an enterprise to $3 billion. He synthesized those experiences into a repeatable, data-driven approach to revenue operations — a science of predictability rather than an art of improvisation.

Despite his focus on landing his next top-tier executive role, White felt what he describes as a "passion-driven obligation" to ensure smaller entrepreneurs aren't left behind by the high cost of modern sales consulting and enterprise SaaS tooling.

The Four Pillars of the PULSE Framework

PULSE is built on four operational pillars that the September 2025 launch brings together into one integrated, free platform:

Pillar One
Revenue Alignment Audit
A step-by-step diagnostic tool for measuring the health of sales fundamentals — without costly software or consulting engagements.
Pillar Two
Performance Synchronization
A methodology for aligning individual sales representative activity with core revenue-generating goals across the whole team.
Pillar Three
Shoestring Budget Scaling
Practical, no-cost go-to-market workflows designed to mirror the discipline of high-scale territory builds at enterprise scale.
Pillar Four
Team Sync Protocols
Simplified communication structures that ensure sales, marketing, and customer success move in a single coordinated rhythm.

Democratizing Enterprise Discipline

White's thesis with PULSE is straightforward: the operational discipline that built $3B enterprises should not be locked behind six-figure software contracts or seven-figure consulting budgets. Every framework, playbook, and tool in PULSE is built to run on a standard laptop, by a single founder, in a single afternoon of setup.

By releasing the entire system for free, White is positioning PULSE as an accessible alternative for the next generation of founders and revenue leaders — and as a living portfolio of his own operating philosophy for the senior executive roles he is actively pursuing.

About Kory J. White
Kory J. White is an executive leader and Chief Revenue Officer with a 22-year track record of scaling high-performance commercial organizations. A graduate of the University of Louisville, White spent his career at Cellular Sales of Knoxville — Verizon's largest authorized retail partner — rising to Regional President and Managing Partner. He architected $200M+ ARR markets, delivered back-to-back 112% quota attainment across 200-person teams, and served as a key leader during the enterprise's scaling to $3 billion in revenue. He resides in the Annapolis, Maryland area.

About PULSE RevOps
PULSE RevOps is a free, open-source revenue operating system designed for startups, SMBs, and growth-stage organizations. Available at pulserevops.com with no signup, no paywall, and no catch.
