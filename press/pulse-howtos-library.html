PULSE RevOps Publishes 39-Industry RevOps Playbook Library — From Home Security to Nonprofit Fundraising

Free, public RevOps reference covers 39 verticals with the tactics that move the number, the structural challenges every operator hits, and how the PULSE framework adapts to each industry's cadence.
⚡ The Hot Take
Most RevOps content is written for SaaS and quietly ignores everyone else. Door-to-door pest control, solar finance fall-out, and veterinary recall all live or die on the same operating disciplines — they just need the metrics translated to their math.
— Kory J. White, on the thesis behind the Industry Playbook library
📅 April 26, 2026 📍 Stevensville, MD ✍️ Kory White 🏷 Content · SEO · Industry Playbooks

STEVENSVILLE, MD — April 26, 2026 — PULSE RevOps, the free revenue operating system built by Chief Revenue Officer Kory J. White, today published a 39-industry RevOps playbook library at pulserevops.com/how-tos/. Each playbook covers a single vertical — from Home Security and SaaS to Pharma, Veterinary, and Nonprofit fundraising — and translates the PULSE framework into the cadence, KPIs, and structural challenges that vertical actually operates against.

The library closes a long-running gap in RevOps content: most modern operating-systems content is written almost exclusively for B2B SaaS, leaving the operators who run trades businesses, retail, healthcare, agriculture, and recurring-service models without a usable reference. PULSE's framework — Pulse Check, Lightning Rounds, and Pulse Points — applies cleanly to every revenue-pipeline business; the playbooks make that translation explicit, vertical by vertical.

"I've spent 22 years running revenue teams across telecom retail, services, and franchise networks. Most RevOps content treats SaaS as the universe and everything else as a footnote. The playbooks fix that. They take the same operating disciplines — pipeline hygiene, leading indicators, comp design, retention math — and translate them into the language a pest-control operator, a hospital revenue cycle leader, or a moving-company GM actually uses." — Kory J. White, Chief Revenue Officer, PULSE Revenue Operations

What's in Each Playbook

Every industry guide follows the same structure, so a leader can move between verticals — or learn an adjacent one — without re-orienting:

Section One
Why this industry is different
A short, honest framing of the revenue physics specific to this category — the buying cycles, customer expectations, and margin structures generic sales advice can't address.
Section Two
The 4 KPIs that matter most
Stop tracking everything. Each playbook names exactly four metrics that give the clearest signal of revenue health — with benchmarks and a Key Insight callout per industry.
Section Three
Live industry news feed
A Google News RSS pull on each page surfaces the five most recent headlines for that vertical — auto-refreshed daily, so the playbook stays a living reference.
Section Four
5 moves to scale without chaos
A numbered list of operating moves — same-day install discipline, cancellation tracking, territory geography, referral cadence — translated to the verbs the operator already uses.
Section Five
"The one thing most leaders miss"
A pull-quote per playbook naming the structural mistake almost every operator in the category makes — surfaced before the framework section so the framework lands harder.
Section Six
PULSE dashboard application
Each guide ends with concrete instructions for using PULSE's Pulse Check, Gross Profit Calculator, Lightning Rounds, Rep Scheduling Matrix, and Recruiting Calculator inside that vertical — plus a 3-question FAQ and a direct CTA into the live dashboard.

The 39 Industries Covered

The library spans high-velocity recurring-revenue verticals (Home Security, Internet, HVAC, Pest Control, Cleaning, MSP), capital-intensive long-cycle verticals (Solar, Mortgage, Construction, Auto), regulated verticals (Healthcare, Pharma, Medical Device, Banking, Insurance, Legal), and operationally-complex verticals (Restaurant, Wholesale, Logistics, Events, Hospitality). Service businesses (Dental, Veterinary, Wellness, Gym, Real Estate) and content/media businesses (Streaming, Digital Advertising, EdTech) are also represented, alongside emerging-pressure categories (Cybersecurity, E-commerce, Staffing) and one mission-driven outlier — Nonprofit fundraising — built around donor retention math.

Why Industry-Specific RevOps Matters

The decision to build out 39 verticals — rather than ship a single "RevOps fundamentals" guide — was deliberate. The same operating principle (track leading indicators, pay reps on the right denominator, structure pipeline cadence around the deal cycle) lands very differently when the deal cycle is 90 minutes versus 90 days. A solar operator's "pull-through" math is closer to a mortgage lender's than to a SaaS team's. A veterinary clinic's recall cadence is closer to an insurance renewal cadence than to a typical CRM motion. Naming those parallels — and the boundaries — is what makes a playbook useful instead of generic.

Each guide is also color-accented to match the industry icon, gives the framework adaptation a concrete entry point, and links directly to the live PULSE War Room dashboards where the same operating disciplines can be observed against sample data.

Pairing Playbooks with the Live Demo

The library ships alongside the existing free PULSE platform — a 40-dashboard War Room, an AI-First Engine that monitors pipeline autonomously, a guided 46-step product tour, and the full operating-system toolset. Playbook readers who want to see the framework in action can move from any industry page directly into the live dashboards in a single click.

The platform remains free, with no signup, no paywall, and no enterprise license required. The complete library is live now at pulserevops.com/how-tos/.
