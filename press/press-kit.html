Long Bio · 220 words

Kory J. White is a Chief Revenue Officer and the architect of PULSE RevOps, a free revenue operating system used by sales leaders, founders, and fractional executives across 47+ countries. Over a 22-year career at Cellular Sales of Knoxville — Verizon's largest authorized retail partner — White rose from individual contributor to Regional President and Managing Partner, ultimately serving as a key leader during the enterprise's scaling to $3 billion in revenue.

In his executive tenure, White architected $200M+ ARR markets across the Mid-Atlantic, delivered back-to-back 112% quota attainment across 200-person field teams, and built operating frameworks that became templates for adjacent regions. His direct experience running revenue organizations through the messy middle of growth — where leaders typically lose their best reps to spreadsheet sprawl and stale reporting — informs every component of the PULSE platform.

White built PULSE as a public demonstration of his operating philosophy: that the modern revenue leader's job is to coach humans, not chase dashboards, and that the tooling required to run a high-performance team should not cost $40,000 a year. The platform is free, login-free, and will remain so. White is based in Stevensville, Maryland, and continues to pursue senior revenue leadership roles while maintaining PULSE as an open work-product.

Talking Points · 5 Story Angles

Angle 1
Free vs. $40K/yr
PULSE deliberately mirrors the UI of Salesforce Lightning and HubSpot as selectable skin modes, letting any sales leader run their workflow on the platform their stakeholders expect to see — without paying for the enterprise license. This raises a real question: what is a CRM seat fee actually paying for?
Angle 2
Operator-Built Tool
Most modern SaaS is built by engineers who have never carried a bag. PULSE is the opposite — every feature was specced by a CRO who managed 200-rep teams and got tired of building the same Sunday-night reports his leaders should have had at 8am Monday.
Angle 3
Fractional CRO Movement
PULSE doubles as a working portfolio for the fractional / consultant revenue leader market — proof that an experienced operator can demonstrate operating philosophy publicly without an NDA shielding the work product. The platform is itself a hiring artifact.
Angle 4
AI in Sales (Done Right)
PULSE's AI-First Engine runs four autonomous subsystems on independent clocks — Auto-Insights every 4 hours, Smart Alerts hourly, Daily Briefing each morning, Proactive Coach every 8 hours. It is among the few free tools demonstrating proactive (vs. reactive) AI in a revenue context.
Angle 5
DMV / Maryland Tech
PULSE was built in Stevensville, Maryland, and serves a specifically named Maryland / Washington DC / DMV revenue-leader audience. Local-tech press angle: a senior revenue executive shipping enterprise-grade software from the Eastern Shore.

Boilerplate · About PULSE RevOps

PULSE RevOps is a free, open revenue operating system designed for Chief Revenue Officers, VPs of Sales, Fractional CROs, and growth-stage founders. The platform includes a 40-dashboard War Room, autonomous AI subsystems, SKU inventory aging intelligence, House Goals × Pulse Check planning matrices, rep scheduling tools, and full Salesforce / HubSpot UI emulation modes. PULSE is built by Kory J. White and is available at pulserevops.com with no signup, no paywall, and no enterprise contract. Based in Stevensville, Maryland.
