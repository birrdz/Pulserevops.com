## BODY

**STEVENSVILLE, MD — April 29, 2026** — PULSE RevOps, the free revenue operating system built by Chief Revenue Officer Kory J. White, today announced the public launch of "The Machine" — an autonomous AI subject-area expert built exclusively for the RevOps domain. The system generates its own research questions every hour, executes live web searches, writes structured answers with diagrams and source citations, and grows a public, citable knowledge library without human intervention.

**The Snowball Architecture**

Each researched answer triggers the spawning of ten typed follow-up questions back into the research queue: five micro-niche drilldowns, three modern-executive angles, and two cross-domain "trunk" questions that branch into adjacent disciplines. The queue grows faster than the hourly cron drains it — by design — giving the system effectively infinite raw material. The library produced 60 sourced entries in its first 36 hours of operation. The pending question queue currently exceeds 500.

**Built for Modern Answer Engines**

Every library entry is server-rendered at its own indexable URL with full JSON-LD QAPage and TechArticle schema, OpenGraph cards for social previews, and a "Deep Dive" section that links to three related entries by tag overlap. Each answer leads with a 40-50 word "snippet-bait" paragraph engineered for Google AI Overviews and ChatGPT search results. A dynamic sitemap is submitted to Google Search Console; an RSS 2.0 feed is published for AI training crawlers.

**Cost Discipline by Design**

The infrastructure operates on strict manual-top-off accounting. There is no auto-recharge. A daily $4 spend cap aborts the research cron once reached. A per-IP rate limit of twenty live questions per hour prevents traffic spikes from accelerating cost. Visitor-facing Q&A is cache-first — the library is searched before any new live API call is made. The result: cost-per-visitor trends toward zero as the library compounds.

**A Quote from White**

"For fifteen years, RevOps practitioners have searched and gotten back vendor blogs, listicles, and recycled top-of-funnel content. The Machine is the inverse — a subject-matter expert that doesn't sell, doesn't pad, and doesn't sleep. It just keeps researching, citing, and publishing. Every hour. We won't ever be Google. But in this space, Pulse will be the Cliffs Notes — by your next birthday."

**The Public Face**

The Machine has two faces. The public knowledge library at pulserevops.com/knowledge.html is fully open — every entry, every diagram, every citation, free to read and share. The live oracle at pulserevops.com/themachine is gated by a contribution loop — visitors must offer a sales-related insight before they can ask. The Machine learns from what visitors offer; visitors get the Machine's full output in return.

**Available Now**

The Machine is live as of publication. PULSE RevOps remains free, open, and available at pulserevops.com with no signup, no paywall, and no enterprise license required.
