PULSE RevOps Launches "The Machine" — A Free, Autonomous AI Knowledge Engine for the RevOps Profession

## SUMMARY (50-200 chars — appears in listings)

## BODY

**STEVENSVILLE, MD, April 29, 2026** — PULSE RevOps, a free public revenue operating system built by Chief Revenue Officer Kory J. White, today announced the launch of "The Machine" — an autonomous AI subject-area expert designed and operated exclusively for the revenue-operations profession. The system independently generates research questions, executes live web searches, writes sourced and structured answers with diagrams, and grows a public, citable knowledge library — all without human input or intervention.

In its first 36 hours of operation, The Machine published 60 fully-sourced library entries spanning sales execution, GTM strategy, SaaS metrics, compensation design, hiring, and pricing governance. Every entry includes a Mermaid diagram visualizing the framework, comparison tables when relevant, and clickable source citations from authoritative web sources.

**The Self-Spawning Library**

Each new answer the Machine produces triggers ten typed follow-up questions back into its own research queue — five micro-niche drilldowns, three modern-executive angles, and two cross-domain "trunk" questions that branch into adjacent revenue disciplines. The queue grows faster than the hourly research cron drains it. As of publication, the queue exceeds 500 pending questions and continues to compound.

"We won't ever be Google," said White. "But in this space, Pulse will be the Cliffs Notes — by your next birthday. The Machine never sleeps. When it's not answering a live visitor, it's hunting the next question every few seconds. There may be 100,000 questions in the RevOps domain. Maybe 250,000. We'll cover them all inside two months."

**Architected for the Modern Answer Engine Era**

Each entry is a standalone indexable URL with QAPage and TechArticle JSON-LD schema, OpenGraph cards for social previews, and a "Deep Dive" cluster of related entries by tag overlap. Every answer leads with a 40-50 word snippet-bait paragraph engineered for Google AI Overviews, Perplexity, and ChatGPT search. The dynamic sitemap is submitted to Google Search Console; an RSS 2.0 feed is published explicitly for ingestion by AI training crawlers.

**Cost-Disciplined by Design**

There is no auto-recharge. Every dollar of Anthropic API spend flows through a manual top-off that the operator funds personally. A daily $4 spend cap aborts the research cron once reached. Per-IP rate limiting prevents traffic spikes from running away with cost. Visitor-facing Q&A is cache-first — the library is searched before any live API call.

**Available Now, Free**

The Machine is live at pulserevops.com/themachine. The public knowledge library is at pulserevops.com/knowledge.html. PULSE RevOps remains free with no signup, no paywall, and no enterprise license.
