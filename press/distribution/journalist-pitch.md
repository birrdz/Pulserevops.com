# Direct Journalist Pitch Template + Target List

**Use case:** Cold-emailing reporters at major outlets to get a real-coverage feature, not just an indexed press release.

**Honest expectation:** Cold pitches get ~1-3% reply rates even at the high end. The 3-5 hours of personalization is the leverage. Send to 12 outlets, expect 1-2 substantive replies, expect 1 piece of coverage if your story is genuinely strong.

**Best send window:** Tue/Wed/Thu, 7-9 AM in the journalist's local time zone. Avoid Mondays (inbox flood) and Fridays (deprioritized).

## SENDING DISCIPLINE

- **Do NOT mass-mail.** Each pitch must reference their specific recent work.
- **Send Tue/Wed/Thu, 7-9 AM their local time.** Mondays = inbox flood. Fridays = deprioritized.
- **One follow-up at 5 days, max.** "Wanted to bump this in case it got buried — happy to send a one-line summary if easier."
- **Track replies in a simple sheet.** Even non-replies are data — adjust the angle for the next batch.
- **Don't ask for "coverage."** Ask for a 15-min call. Reporters say yes to calls. They say no to "coverage."
