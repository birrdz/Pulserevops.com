## How to Choose / What Matters

- **Construction decides longevity.** Goodyear-welted shoes (Allen Edmonds, Meermin, Loake) can be resoled and last decades; cemented soles can't.
- **Start with one versatile pair.** A black cap-toe oxford covers the most formal needs; a dark-brown derby or loafer covers business casual.
- **Let age guide the pairing.** Younger pros can lean into sharper, slimmer tailoring; senior leaders pair the same welted classics with relaxed, distinguished cuts.
- **Full-grain leather** ages best and takes a polish — avoid corrected-grain or coated finishes that crack.
- **Buy two and rotate.** Letting shoes rest a day between wears dries out moisture and dramatically extends their life.

## What to Avoid

- Square-toed or overly long "banana" toe shapes — they date instantly.
- Glued, non-resoleable soles on anything you want to keep for years.
- Matching brown shoes with a black belt (or vice versa) — leather should coordinate.
- Wearing the same pair every single day with no rest or cedar shoe trees.

## FAQ

### What's the single best first dress shoe for a man?
A black cap-toe oxford like the Allen Edmonds Park Avenue (~$425) or, on a budget, the Meermin Linea Maestro (~$190). It's the most formal silhouette and pairs with navy, charcoal, and gray suits, so it covers interviews, weddings, and the boardroom in one shoe.

### How should work shoes change as a man ages?
The classics stay the same, but the pairing evolves. In your 20s, a derby or loafer under slim tailoring reads modern and ambitious. In your 40s, a welted cap-toe oxford with a charcoal suit projects authority. In your 60s, a burnished brown oxford under a classic navy suit reads distinguished and timeless.

### Can women wear men's-style dress shoes to work?
Yes — sleek leather loafers, pointed oxfords, and chelsea boots in women's sizing look sharp and modern under trousers, skirts, and dresses. They're often more comfortable than heels for long days, and a closed-toe pump remains the dressier option when needed.

### How do I keep work dress shoes looking new?
Use cedar shoe trees after each wear to absorb moisture and hold shape, rotate between two pairs, brush and condition the leather regularly, and polish before they look dull. Resole welted shoes when the soles wear through rather than replacing the whole shoe.

The best men's work dress shoes are welted, full-grain leather oxfords, derbies, loafers, and chelsea boots — the Allen Edmonds Park Avenue and value-priced Meermin lead the pack — and one versatile black or brown pair covers most offices.
