# Best Cadillac XT5 Model Years (Ranked)

The Cadillac XT5 launched for 2017 as the replacement for the SRX and quickly became Cadillac's best-selling vehicle, a midsize luxury crossover that blends a roomy two-row cabin, a comfortable ride, and a choice of a **3.6L V6** or a later **2.0L turbocharged four-cylinder**. Trims span **Luxury, Premium Luxury, and Sport**, with front- or all-wheel drive. A meaningful **2020 mid-cycle refresh** reworked the styling, infotainment, and engine lineup, splitting the XT5's life into clear pre- and post-refresh eras. Choosing the right model year means weighing the smoother newer interior against earlier value, plus a few known quirks. This ranking covers the best XT5 years, their powertrains, and where the value lies on the used market today.

## Direct Answer

The **best overall Cadillac XT5 is the 2021-2023 model**, which combines the improved 2020-refresh interior and revised **CUE infotainment** with the strongest reliability record, the available 235-hp **2.0L turbo** or smooth **3.6L V6**, and the most modern driver-assistance suite. For shoppers focused on value, the **best value is the 2018-2019 Premium Luxury V6**, which delivers the proven 310-hp 3.6L V6, leather, and a well-equipped cabin at a steep used discount while sidestepping the first-year 2017 teething issues. Avoid leaning on the earliest 2017 cars unless the price is very low, since the pre-refresh infotainment and eight-speed automatic drew the most complaints.

## 1. 2021-2023 Cadillac XT5 🏆 BEST OVERALL

The post-refresh XT5 is the model at its best. The **2020 refresh** brought a cleaner front fascia, a redesigned interior with a **rotary controller and 8-inch touchscreen**, and standard wireless **Apple CarPlay and Android Auto**. By 2021-2023 the lineup had settled, offering a **235-hp 2.0L turbo four** as standard on lower trims and the familiar **310-hp 3.6L V6** on Premium Luxury and Sport. **Reliability is the strongest of any XT5 era**, and the available **Super Cruise-adjacent driver aids** and quieter cabin make these the most refined examples. If your budget reaches a recent used XT5, this is the one to buy for the best blend of comfort, tech, and dependability.

## 2. 2018-2019 Premium Luxury V6 💎 BEST VALUE

The pre-refresh **3.6L V6** XT5 in Premium Luxury trim is the value champion. By 2018-2019 Cadillac had **resolved most of the 2017 first-year bugs**, and the **310-hp V6** with its eight-speed automatic delivers smooth, effortless power and a 0-60 time around 6.7 seconds. **The best value is a 2018-2019 Premium Luxury**, which bundles leather, a panoramic sunroof, heated seats, and a strong safety package at a used price well below newer rivals like the Lexus RX or Audi Q5. The ride is comfortable and quiet, and the V6 is durable when maintained. For buyers who want genuine luxury on a budget, this era is hard to beat.

## 3. 2023 Cadillac XT5 Sport

The **Sport trim** earns its own spot for delivering the most engaging XT5 experience. It pairs the **310-hp 3.6L V6** with standard all-wheel drive, **continuous damping control**, and a sportier suspension tune, plus blacked-out exterior trim and unique wheels. The 2023 model year benefits from the most mature version of the refreshed interior and the longest remaining factory warranty among used examples. **The driving feel is noticeably tauter** than Luxury trims without sacrificing the XT5's signature comfort. Reliability remains strong. Buy this one if you want the best-handling, best-equipped XT5 and are comfortable paying near the top of the used range for a low-mileage car.

## 4. 2020 Cadillac XT5 (Refreshed)

The **2020 model year introduced the mid-cycle refresh**, making it the first XT5 with the updated interior, the new **2.0L turbo** base engine option, and standard wireless smartphone integration. It carries the freshened exterior styling and improved infotainment that define the better second half of the XT5's life. As the first refreshed year it has a slightly shorter track record than 2021-2023, but **early reliability has been solid**. For a buyer who wants the modern interior and tech at a lower price than the newest cars, a clean 2020 XT5 in Premium Luxury trim is a smart, well-rounded choice with a proven V6 available.

## 5. 2018-2019 Cadillac XT5 Luxury (Base) V6

The base **Luxury trim** of the 2018-2019 XT5 offers the same dependable **310-hp 3.6L V6** and comfortable ride as the Premium Luxury at an even lower used price. It comes well-equipped with **dual-zone climate, leatherette or leather, and a generous touchscreen**, though it omits some of the higher trims' advanced safety tech and trim upgrades. **The value here is real** for buyers who do not need every option. These cars share the same proven mechanicals and post-first-year refinement. A clean Luxury V6 makes an excellent affordable entry into Cadillac ownership, with the V6's smoothness being the standout feature at this price.

## 6. 2021-2023 Cadillac XT5 2.0T

The **2.0L turbocharged four-cylinder** introduced on lower trims is a sensible choice for buyers prioritizing efficiency. It makes **235 horsepower and 258 lb-ft of torque**, paired with a nine-speed automatic, and returns better fuel economy than the V6 while still moving the XT5 adequately. **It is best suited to lighter-footed driving**, as it lacks the V6's effortless surge, but for commuting it is more than capable. The post-refresh interior and tech come standard. These turbo-four examples often sell at a small discount to V6 cars, making them a reasonable pick for efficiency-focused shoppers who want the newer XT5 at a lower entry price.

## 7. 2020 Cadillac XT5 2.0T

The **2020 model year was the first to offer the 2.0L turbo four** as the entry engine, replacing the underpowered 3.6L base configuration logic with a more efficient option. It pairs the **235-hp turbo** with the nine-speed automatic and the freshened interior. As the first year for this powertrain in the XT5, it has a slightly shorter reliability history than 2021-2023, but no major engine concerns have emerged. **It is a budget-friendly way** into the refreshed XT5 for buyers who value fuel economy over outright power. Confirm maintenance on the turbo system and look for a clean example in Premium Luxury trim for the best equipment.

## 8. 2017 Cadillac XT5 Premium Luxury (First Year)

The **2017 XT5 was the launch year**, replacing the SRX with a roomier, lighter crossover and the **310-hp 3.6L V6**. The Premium Luxury trim is the best-equipped first-year choice, with leather, a panoramic roof, and a comprehensive safety suite. However, **2017 cars drew the most complaints** about the pre-refresh **CUE infotainment** and occasional rough shifts from the eight-speed automatic. The styling and mechanicals are sound, and prices are now very low. Buy a 2017 only with documented service history and a careful inspection of the transmission and electronics. For most buyers the 2018-2019 cars are a safer, more refined alternative.

## 9. 2017 Cadillac XT5 Luxury (First Year, Caution)

The base **Luxury trim of the 2017 XT5** is the most affordable XT5 on the used market, but it combines the first-year teething issues with fewer features. It uses the same **310-hp 3.6L V6** and eight-speed automatic, so the **pre-refresh infotainment glitches and occasional harsh shifts** apply here too. The cabin is comfortable but more basic, missing some of the advanced safety tech that became more common later. **Only buy at a low price with full documentation** that any infotainment or transmission software updates were performed. Otherwise the smoother, better-equipped 2018-2019 Luxury V6 is well worth the modest step up in price.

## 10. 2017-2019 Cadillac XT5 FWD Base Configurations

The **front-wheel-drive base configurations** of the early XT5 round out the list. They share the **3.6L V6** but skip all-wheel drive and the higher trims' equipment, making them the cheapest path into an XT5. **FWD reduces traction in snow** compared with the available AWD system, which matters for cold-climate buyers, and these stripped configurations lack many comfort and safety extras. The upside is rock-bottom pricing. These are best treated as budget luxury transportation rather than fully loaded examples. For buyers in mild climates who simply want a comfortable, smooth V6 crossover at the lowest possible cost, a clean FWD base car can make sense.

## What to Watch For When Buying

The single most important step when buying a used XT5 is to **decide between the pre- and post-2020-refresh eras**, since the refresh substantially improved the infotainment and interior. On **2017 first-year cars**, scrutinize the **CUE infotainment system** for glitches and freezes, and confirm the **eight-speed automatic** shifts smoothly without harshness, as these were the most common early complaints. Run the VIN through Cadillac's recall lookup and the NHTSA database to confirm any software updates were completed. **Choosing the 3.6L V6 over the 2.0L turbo** gives smoother, more effortless power and a longer proven track record, which is why V6 models earn the most trust. Beyond the powertrain, inspect the panoramic sunroof seals for leaks, verify the all-wheel-drive system engages properly, and prioritize a documented maintenance history over a low sticker price.

## How to Choose

Match the XT5 to your priorities. For **the best blend of refinement, technology, and reliability**, the 2021-2023 post-refresh model is the answer, with the V6 leading on smoothness and the 2.0T on efficiency. For **the best value with proven durability**, a 2018-2019 Premium Luxury V6 is hard to beat, offering genuine luxury at a steep used discount after the first-year bugs were resolved. Buyers wanting **the sportiest experience** should target a 2023 Sport with continuous damping control. Bargain hunters can consider 2017 cars or FWD base configurations, but should always inspect the infotainment and transmission and favor the V6. In every case, confirm recall completion and prioritize a clean service history.

## FAQ

**Which Cadillac XT5 years should I avoid?**
Be cautious with the **2017 first-year cars**, which drew the most complaints about the pre-refresh CUE infotainment and occasional rough eight-speed automatic shifts. The later 2018-2019 and post-refresh 2020-2023 models are more refined and trouble-free.

**What changed in the 2020 Cadillac XT5 refresh?**
The 2020 mid-cycle refresh updated the exterior styling, redesigned the interior with a new rotary controller and improved infotainment, added standard wireless Apple CarPlay and Android Auto, and introduced the 235-hp 2.0L turbo four as a base engine option.

**Is the XT5's 2.0L turbo or 3.6L V6 better?**
The 310-hp 3.6L V6 offers smoother, more effortless power and the longest proven track record, making it the better choice for most buyers. The 235-hp 2.0L turbo trades some power for better fuel economy and a lower entry price.

**Which XT5 is best for luxury on a budget?**
A 2018-2019 Premium Luxury V6 offers leather, a panoramic roof, the smooth V6, and a strong safety package at a used price well below newer rivals, making it the smart value pick after the first-year issues were resolved.

## Bottom Line

The Cadillac XT5 is a comfortable, well-equipped midsize luxury crossover whose **2020 refresh makes era and model-year choice critical**. The **2021-2023 post-refresh model is the best overall pick**, with the improved interior, modern tech, and the strongest reliability, while the **2018-2019 Premium Luxury V6 offers the best value** once the first-year bugs were sorted. When shopping any early car, favor the **3.6L V6** and inspect the CUE infotainment and transmission closely. Buy carefully and the XT5 delivers genuine Cadillac comfort, a roomy cabin, and dependable performance at a price that undercuts most luxury rivals.
