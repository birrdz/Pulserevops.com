## 1. Where Iowa Stands — Ferentz Era 2027 NIL Math

Iowa is the only Power 4 program where the head coach in 2027 will be older than the program's NIL collective. Kirk Ferentz turns 72 during the 2027 season. He told reporters in May that he "doesn't envision stopping in the near future" and his contract runs through 2029, but every recruit's parent is doing the same math: who coaches my kid in year three? That uncertainty is the single biggest drag on Iowa NIL valuations right now, and it shows up in collective fundraising. The Swarm Collective opened its membership to fund all 22 sports in 2024 and added branded "Swarm Water" through Iowa packer Crystal Clear, but ED Scott Brickman has publicly conceded the collective is behind on its football goal. Meanwhile Iowa Athletics, under AD Beth Goetz, hit the full $20.5M House settlement cap for 2025-26 and stood up Flight Funds as a direct donor channel for revenue share. Football's slice of that cap typically runs 70-75% at Big Ten peers, meaning the 2027 football revenue-share line is roughly $14-15.5M before collective dollars. Iowa also hired former NFL GM Scott Pioli as a revenue-share consultant. The roster math is unforgiving — Mark Gronowski leaves after 2025, Jeremy Hecklinski is the projected 2026 starter, and 2027 needs a real QB1 contract.

## 2. Real 2027 Strategy — 5 Moves

**Move 1 — Stand up a named QB Fund inside Swarm.** Iowa's offense has been the punchline of Big Ten Twitter for a decade. The fix is not a coordinator hire, it is a dedicated, transparent $2.5M QB pool donors can write checks to specifically. Tennessee and Texas A&M proved that named position funds raise more than general appeals.

**Move 2 — Lock the offensive line for two cycles.** Iowa's OL pipeline is the program's actual brand. Use 18-22% of the 2027 collective spend on multi-year OL deals with clear renewal triggers. The OL room is where Ferentz still recruits at an elite level, and it is the cheapest place to outbid Nebraska and Wisconsin.

**Move 3 — Publish a succession bridge memo.** Donors will not max-fund a 72-year-old coach without knowing the plan. Beth Goetz should publicly name the search committee structure and Scott Pioli's role in coach-transition cap modeling. Vague is fatal here.

**Move 4 — Monetize the Kinnick tailgate economy.** Iowa City sells out 69,250 seats every Saturday and the donor base skews farm and business owner. Swarm should bundle game-day hospitality, branded Swarm Water distribution at Casey's and Hy-Vee, and rev-share tiers into one $1,000-and-up product. Volume, not whales.

**Move 5 — Pre-clear every football deal with Deloitte NIL Go.** The clearinghouse is auditing all $600+ third-party deals for fair market value. Iowa's compliance reputation is an asset — lean into it, publish a "Swarm-cleared" badge for athlete deals, and use it as a recruiting differentiator against collectives running gray-area contracts.

## 3. Top 3 Risks

**Risk 1 — The Ferentz transition lands in 2028, not 2030.** The contract runs through 2029, but health, family, or a season collapse could pull the retirement forward. If Iowa is mid-2027 cycle with $20M committed and no announced succession plan, recruits will decommit en masse and the portal will become a one-way exit. Mitigation: publish the bridge plan in fall 2026, not after the fact. Brian Ferentz is no longer on staff after his 2023 OC removal, so the internal heir-apparent narrative is gone — Iowa needs an external short list named privately to donors.

**Risk 2 — Swarm Collective hits a fundraising ceiling.** Brickman has already admitted Swarm is behind on its football goal. Iowa's donor base is broad but not deep — there are no oil-money mega-donors like at Texas or Oklahoma. If the $5-6M football target slips to $3-4M, Iowa will lose every contested portal QB and skill-position recruit in 2027. Mitigation: convert season-ticket holders into auto-recurring Swarm members at the $100/month tier, and lean on Iowa-based corporate sponsors (Casey's, Hy-Vee, Principal Financial, Rockwell Collins) for multi-year underwriting deals routed through NIL Go.

**Risk 3 — NIL Go clearinghouse blocks Iowa deals at higher rates than SEC peers.** The Deloitte fair-market-value model uses national comparables, which can penalize Big Ten programs with smaller local media markets. If Iowa submits a $1.5M QB deal and NIL Go knocks it to $900K based on Cedar Rapids market data, Iowa loses the QB to an SEC school whose comparable deal cleared. Mitigation: structure deals with documented national brand activation (Swarm Water national rollout, Big Ten Network appearances, alumni-network endorsements) so the FMV file shows clear interstate value, not just Iowa City reach.

A: Yes. Ferentz confirmed in May 2026 he will return for his 28th season, and his contract runs through 2029. He told reporters he doesn't envision stopping soon and has his wife Mary's support.

**Q: Who is Iowa's 2026 starting quarterback?**
A: Per ESPN's projection, Jeremy Hecklinski takes over after Mark Gronowski's one-and-done 2025. Hank Brown is the backup, Tradon Bessinger likely redshirts.
