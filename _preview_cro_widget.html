What's the most reliable way to scale a revenue team in 2027?

Direct answer: Install repeatable revenue operations before you add headcount, so every new rep plugs into a system instead of improvising.

Build the operating system first

Define your funnel stages, entry and exit criteria, and the metrics that matter before hiring. A team that shares one definition of a qualified opportunity forecasts far more accurately than one where every rep keeps their own scorecard.

With process and data in place, tooling amplifies instead of masking gaps. Pick systems that enforce your stage definitions and surface the two or three metrics that actually predict revenue.
