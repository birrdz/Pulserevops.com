# Best Cadillac XT6 Model Years (Ranked)

The Cadillac XT6 arrived for the **2020 model year** as Cadillac's three-row midsize luxury crossover, slotting above the two-row XT5 and below the truck-based Escalade. Built on the same GM C1 platform as the XT5 and Chevrolet Blazer, it offers seating for six or seven, a standard **3.6L V6** and a later available **2.0L turbo four-cylinder**, and trims spanning **Luxury, Premium Luxury, and Sport**. It has never been a class leader for driving excitement, but it delivers a quiet, comfortable cabin, available all-wheel drive, and Cadillac's strong **Super Cruise** hands-free tech on later cars. This ranking covers the best XT6 model years, their powertrains, known issues, and where the used value lies today.

## Direct Answer

The **best overall Cadillac XT6 is the 2023-2024 model**, which benefits from a refreshed exterior, an updated dual-screen interior, standard advanced safety tech, and availability of Cadillac's excellent **Super Cruise** hands-free driving system, all backed by the most remaining factory warranty. For shoppers focused on price, the **best value is the 2021-2022 model with the 3.6L V6 in Premium Luxury trim**, which adds the standard 9-speed automatic refinement and expanded standard safety equipment from the 2021 update while costing far less than a new example. The first-year **2020** is the cheapest entry point but carries early-production risk; verify recalls and software updates on any year.

## 1. 2023-2024 XT6 🏆 BEST OVERALL

The **2023-2024 XT6** is the most complete version of the car. The **2022 mid-cycle refresh** carried forward here brought sharper front-end styling, vertical LED lighting, and a modernized cabin centered on a larger reconfigured infotainment setup with improved usability. Both the **3.6L V6 (310 hp)** and the **2.0L turbo four (235 hp)** pair with a 9-speed automatic, and the available **Super Cruise** hands-free highway system is a genuine standout in the segment. Standard driver-assistance content expanded, and build quality feels the most polished of the run. As the newest used examples, these carry the longest remaining warranty and the lowest depreciation risk, making them the XT6 to buy if your budget reaches a recent car.

## 2. 2021-2022 XT6 Premium Luxury — 3.6L V6 💎 BEST VALUE

The **2021-2022 XT6** is the value sweet spot. The **2021 model year** brought meaningful upgrades: the **2.0L turbo** joined the lineup, more driver-assistance features became standard, and Cadillac expanded available tech. The dependable **3.6L V6 (310 hp)** with the 9-speed automatic delivers smooth, quiet power and tows up to 4,000 lbs when equipped. **The best value is a 2021-2022 Premium Luxury V6**, which bundles leather, heated seats, and a full suite of safety gear at a used price well below a new XT6. These cars are recent enough to feel current yet have shed the steepest depreciation, offering near-flagship comfort for substantially less money.

## 3. 2022 XT6 (Refreshed)

The **2022 model year** introduced the XT6's **mid-cycle refresh**, with revised exterior styling, updated lighting signatures, and an improved interior layout. It is functionally the bridge between the original 2020-2021 design and the polished 2023-2024 cars, sharing the newer look at a slightly lower used price. Both the **V6** and **2.0L turbo** were offered, paired with the 9-speed automatic, and **Super Cruise** became available on properly equipped models. For a buyer who wants the freshened design and tech without paying the newest-car premium, a clean 2022 example is a smart, well-rounded pick that already feels modern inside and out.

## 4. 2021 XT6 — 3.6L V6

The **2021 XT6** is a strong, low-risk choice that improves on the first-year car. The **2021 update** added the 2.0L turbo option, expanded standard safety equipment, and refined the available technology while keeping the proven **3.6L V6** and 9-speed automatic. It retains the original pre-refresh exterior, which keeps prices reasonable, but mechanically and electronically it addresses early teething concerns. The cabin is quiet and comfortable with three rows of seating and available all-wheel drive. For value-focused buyers who want the dependable V6 and updated standard content at a lower price than the refreshed cars, the 2021 V6 is an excellent target.

## 5. 2023 XT6 Sport — 3.6L V6

Powered by the **3.6L V6 (310 hp)** with the 9-speed automatic and standard all-wheel drive, it delivers the firmest, most engaging XT6 driving experience while keeping the upgraded 2023 interior and available **Super Cruise**. It is not a true performance SUV, but the Sport's styling and chassis tuning give it the most distinctive personality in the lineup. Buyers who want the freshened design with a sharper edge and the V6's smooth power will find the 2023 Sport a desirable used pick.

## 6. 2024 XT6 — 2.0L Turbo

The **2024 XT6 with the 2.0L turbo four-cylinder (235 hp)** is the newest, most efficient way into the lineup. The turbo four returns better city fuel economy than the V6 while still moving the three-row crossover adequately for daily duty, and it carries all the refreshed 2023-2024 styling, interior tech, and standard safety content. It has the shortest track record and the most remaining warranty. Buy this one if efficiency and the latest design matter more than maximum power; for towing or effortless highway passing, the **V6 remains the better engine**. As a near-new car, it commands the highest used pricing in the family.

## 7. 2022 XT6 — 2.0L Turbo

The turbo's character is adequate rather than effortless, and the heavier three-row body asks a lot of it under load, but in routine driving it works fine. With the freshened 2022 design at a lower price than a comparable V6 model, this is a sensible used choice for efficiency-minded shoppers who still want the newer look and feature set.

## What to Watch For When Buying

The single most important step when buying a used XT6 is to **run the VIN through Cadillac's recall lookup and the NHTSA database**, especially on first-year **2020** cars, and confirm that all service campaigns and **infotainment software updates** were completed. Early CUE infotainment glitches and software-related quirks were the most common owner complaints, and most were addressed through dealer updates. Beyond that, **the 3.6L V6 is the more proven, more capable engine**, with smoother power and higher tow capacity than the 2.0L turbo, so it earns the most trust on the used market. Inspect the 9-speed automatic for smooth shifts, verify all driver-assistance and Super Cruise features function on equipped cars, and check for normal wear on higher-mileage examples. Documented maintenance always outweighs a slightly lower sticker price.

## How to Choose

Match the XT6 to your priorities. For **the best blend of modern design, tech, and warranty**, the 2023-2024 cars are the answer, with **Super Cruise** the standout feature for highway drivers. For **the best value with proven mechanicals**, a 2021-2022 3.6L V6 in Premium Luxury is hard to beat, pairing the updated standard safety content with a quiet, comfortable cabin at a strong used price. Buyers wanting the **refreshed look without the newest-car premium** should target a 2022. Efficiency-minded shoppers can consider the **2.0L turbo**, but anyone who tows or wants effortless power should favor the **V6**. In every case, verify recall and software-update completion through the VIN.

## FAQ

**Which Cadillac XT6 years should I avoid?**
The first-year **2020** is the riskiest, mainly due to early infotainment software quirks and launch-era teething issues. It can still be a bargain if the VIN confirms all recalls and software updates were completed; otherwise step up to a 2021-or-newer car.

**Is the 3.6L V6 or the 2.0L turbo better in the XT6?**
The **3.6L V6 (310 hp)** is the better all-around engine, offering smoother power, higher tow capacity, and a longer track record. The **2.0L turbo (235 hp)** returns better city fuel economy but feels worked harder in the heavier three-row body.

**What is Super Cruise on the XT6?**
**Super Cruise** is Cadillac's hands-free driver-assistance system for compatible divided highways, using cameras and mapping to steer, accelerate, and brake. It became available on properly equipped later XT6 models and is one of the car's strongest selling points.

**Which XT6 is the best value used?**
A **2021-2022 3.6L V6 in Premium Luxury** offers the updated standard safety content, the proven V6, and a quiet, comfortable cabin at a price well below a new example, making it the smart value pick.

## Bottom Line

The Cadillac XT6 is a quiet, comfortable three-row luxury crossover that makes its strongest case as a used buy. The **2023-2024 models are the best overall pick**, with refreshed styling, updated tech, and available **Super Cruise**, while a **2021-2022 3.6L V6 Premium Luxury offers the best value**. Across every year, favor the **proven V6** over the 2.0L turbo unless efficiency is the priority, and always confirm recall and infotainment software-update completion through the VIN. Choose carefully and the XT6 delivers genuine Cadillac comfort and three-row space for far less than its original sticker.
