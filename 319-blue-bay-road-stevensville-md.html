About 319 Blue Bay Road

Nestled within the prestigious Wolf Farm community, this exquisite residence offers a harmonious blend of luxury and comfort, perfect for those seeking an elevated lifestyle. This property is one of the few on Kent Island with unobstructed views of Annapolis, Baltimore, and the Chesapeake Bay Bridge. Guests are welcomed with a gated circular drive adorned with stone pillars, lush flower beds, and a professionally manicured lawn. The neoclassical architectural style mimics Thomas Jefferson’s Monticello, spanning an impressive 10,152 square feet.

The kitchen has been meticulously renovated in 2026, ensuring convenience while gazing at the Bay. Step inside to discover an open floor plan that invites natural light to dance across stunning marble and luxury vinyl plank flooring. The eat-in kitchen and separate formal dining room provide ample space for entertaining, while the pantry ensures all your culinary needs are met. The primary suite is a true sanctuary — a bay-front view, lavish primary bath, and expansive walk-in closets. Each of the nine bedrooms offers generous space and thoughtful details. Eight full bathrooms ensure morning routines flow smoothly with privacy and convenience for all.

The heart of the home is complemented by three elegant fireplaces, creating cozy nooks for relaxation. Recessed lighting and upgraded countertops add a touch of sophistication, while the wet/dry bar and custom wine cellar elevate the entertaining experience.

Step outside to your personal oasis: a stunning in-ground pool surrounded by multiple terraces and balconies overlooking the serene Chesapeake Bay. With 500 feet of waterfrontage, this property offers unparalleled access to the water, complete with a private composite dock that accommodates multiple slips for your boating adventures. Whether you enjoy power boating or kayaking, the navigable waters provide endless opportunities for exploration.

Additional features include a full daylight basement with outside access, perfect for guests or recreational activities, and an attached side-entry garage with ample driveway space for up to 15 vehicles. This home is not just a residence; it’s a lifestyle. Experience the tranquility of waterfront living combined with the luxury of modern amenities, all within the embrace of a warm and inviting atmosphere.
