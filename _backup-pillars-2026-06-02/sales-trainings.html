What's inside every Pulse training

Each entry is a real, runnable 60-minute sales meeting. Six fixed sections every time:

  • Meeting agenda with minute-marked timing (5 min open · 15 min teach · 15 min discuss · 20 min role-play · 5 min commitments)
  • The "why this matters" cold open — the trigger story or stat that makes the room lean in
  • The teach block — the framework, the 7-question or 5-step or 3-trap structure the reps will leave with
  • Two-person role-play scripts — both sides written out so any pair can run them; the buyer side has the hard objections built in
  • The discussion prompts — what the manager asks the room after the role-play
  • The one-page leave-behind — printable cheat-sheet with the framework, the language, and the commitment they made

Use them as-is, fork them for your stack, or assemble a 12-week curriculum from the library. They're free, indexed, and built to be linked.
