What's inside every Pulse KPI guide

Each entry is a complete, industry-specific KPI playbook. The same fixed structure every time:

  • The 9 KPIs that matter — the metrics that genuinely drive revenue in this industry, not a generic dashboard dump
  • Benchmark ranges — what "bad / okay / good / elite" looks like for each number, with the data sourced and dated
  • How to calculate it — the exact formula, the inputs, and where to pull them from your stack
  • What the number is telling you — how to read each KPI and the failure mode it exposes when it drifts
  • The levers that move it — the concrete operating actions that improve the metric, ranked by impact
  • The one-page scorecard — printable summary with every KPI, its benchmark, and its formula in one place

Use them as-is, fork them for your stack, or stack several into a full revenue scorecard. They're free, indexed, and built to be linked.
