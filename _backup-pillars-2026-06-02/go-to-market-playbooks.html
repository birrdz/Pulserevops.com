What lives here

Go-To-Market Playbooks is the operator desk of Pulse — runnable, step-by-step GTM plans for the moves that actually move a number. The recurring topics:

  • ICP launch playbooks — the first 90 days of a brand-new segment, from list-build and messaging tests to first 10 logos and the trip-wires that say "kill it"
  • PLG → sales-led conversion playbooks — bolting a sales motion onto a self-serve funnel without spooking the flywheel, PQL routing, when to gate features, how to comp the AE
  • Inbound → outbound expansion playbooks — spinning up an outbound engine when inbound stalls, list architecture, sequence cadences, SDR ramp, attribution that survives
  • Regional expansion (US → EU/APAC) playbooks — first hire in-region, entity vs EOR, legal/tax/GDPR, time-zone coverage, comp localization, channel partner sequencing
  • Channel + partner playbooks — direct-to-channel transition, partner-tier design, deal-reg + MDF mechanics, partner-sourced vs partner-influenced pipeline, conflict resolution
  • Pricing repackage + tier overhaul — re-tiering without churning the bottom quartile, grandfathering math, value-metric swap, packaging tests, comp-plan ripple effects
  • Comp + quota redesign mid-year — when the old plan stopped paying for the right behavior, plan economics, accelerator restructure, quota relief, comp-committee survival
  • Sales motion shift (transactional → enterprise) — upmarket move-up, AE persona swap, deal-desk standup, MEDDPICC adoption, cycle-time expansion, forecast retrain
  • Layoff recovery + rebuild playbooks — first 30 days after RIF, surviving-rep stabilization, account redistribution, quota reset, hiring backfill sequence, culture rebuild
  • Land + expand + multi-product expansion — designing for expansion at land, post-sale handoff, expansion playbook by persona, multi-product cross-sell sequencing, NRR architecture
  • Fractional CRO 90-day plans — diagnostic week, listening tour, the first three changes, board-day-one report, what to fix in 30, what to leave for 90

Use them to launch a new motion from a clean sheet, repair a stalled one mid-quarter, or pressure-test a pivot before you take it to the board. Every playbook is opinionated, defensible, and runnable — the goal is to be the resource a sitting CRO would actually keep open in a tab on day one.
