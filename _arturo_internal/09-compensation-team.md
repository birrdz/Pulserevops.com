# Arturo — Compensation & Team (Internal Research Brief)

> For Kory White's job prep. Internal only — NOT for publication. Facts only, sourced inline.
> Arturo is **defunct** (ceased operations 2025). All figures below are historical / past tense.

## Bottom line up front

**Detailed, verified compensation data for Arturo is essentially NOT public.** As a
venture-backed private company (~$47M raised, never public, now defunct), Arturo never had
to disclose salary bands, and third-party salary trackers hold little to no Arturo-specific
data. Below is everything that could actually be sourced, with the gaps stated plainly.

## Headcount over time (what IS known)

Arturo was a small-to-mid-size startup. Employee-count estimates from data aggregators
(note: these are self-reported / scraped and several are now stale snapshots of a defunct
company):

- **Dealroom:** "51–200" employees band. ([dealroom.co](https://app.dealroom.co/companies/arturo))
- **PitchBook:** ~63 total employees (profile figure). ([pitchbook.com](https://pitchbook.com/profiles/company/267695-74))
- **Tracxn:** ~53 employees on its last snapshot (dated Apr 2026 — a stale reading of an already-defunct company). ([tracxn.com](https://tracxn.com/d/companies/arturo/__SIvY3LitHDaBkYFggt0RH64rIkt6wL-Xdb6gqSeF1zQ))
- **Growth note:** aggregators state headcount "more than tripled" since ~March 2020 — consistent with the 2020 Series A ($8M) and 2021 Series B ($25M) funding-fueled hiring. ([cbinsights.com](https://www.cbinsights.com/company/arturo-1))

So a reasonable read: Arturo peaked somewhere in the **low-to-mid dozens up to ~200**
depending on source and date, most likely in the **~50–100 range** at its core, before
winding down. Exact peak headcount is **not reliably documented publicly.**

## Compensation — what little is public

**Glassdoor** is the only mainstream source with any Arturo pay/team data, and it is thin:

- Company rating **3.1 / 5** across ~33–42 reviews; **47%** would recommend to a friend.
- Compensation & benefits sub-rating **3.4 / 5**; work-life balance 3.6, culture/values 3.0, career opportunities 3.1.
- A single surfaced salary data point: **~$134,676/yr average for a Software Engineer** (Glassdoor estimate, small sample). No verified bands for other roles.
- Reviews cite pros (management, career development, benefits) and cons (senior leadership, culture) — including at least one strongly negative review titled "Toxic and destructive culture."
- Source: [Glassdoor – Arturo salaries](https://www.glassdoor.com/Salary/Arturo-Salaries-E2967463.htm) and [Glassdoor – Arturo reviews](https://www.glassdoor.com/Reviews/Arturo-Reviews-E2967463.htm) (pages are login/anti-bot gated; figures above are from search-index snippets).

**Caveat on the SWE figure:** it is a Glassdoor modeled estimate from a very small sample,
not a disclosed band. Treat it as directional only, not a quotable salary.

## Sources checked that returned NO Arturo comp data

- **Levels.fyi:** no Arturo company profile or submitted compensation entries found. Arturo is not in its directory. ([levels.fyi](https://www.levels.fyi/companies))
- **Comparably:** no substantive Arturo compensation/culture profile surfaced.
- **News / trade press** (The Insurer, Coverager, Insurtech Insights): coverage focuses on
  funding, product, clients, and the 2025 shutdown/asset sale — **no salary or comp-structure
  reporting.** No public record of a specific Arturo layoff event with headcount numbers
  (distinct from the broader 2023 insurtech layoff wave, in which ~47% of insurtechs cut staff). ([theinsurer.com](https://www.theinsurer.com/ti/news/adrian-jones-insurtech-headcount-5-in-23-but-over-half-of-firms-reduced-workforces/))

## Team / structure notes (from verified facts + public profiles)

- Leadership turnover was notable: **three CEOs** across its life — co-founder **John-Isaac "JC" Clark** (ex-DigitalGlobe), then a second CEO, then **Marty Smuin** (CEO from Sept 2023 through closure).
- Core functions were engineering/ML (computer vision on satellite/aerial/multispectral imagery), data science, product, and insurance-domain sales/partnerships.
- Locations: Denver, CO originally (4643 S. Ulster St.); relocated to South Jordan / Salt Lake City, Utah (Nov 2024) before winding down in 2025. LinkedIn company page: [linkedin.com/company/arturoai](https://www.linkedin.com/company/arturoai).

## Industry comp context (for calibration, NOT Arturo-specific)

Because Arturo bands aren't public, the useful benchmark is general US insurtech/ML-startup
pay: mid-to-senior software/ML engineers at VC-backed insurtechs typically fell in roughly
the **$120K–$190K base** range in 2021–2024, plus equity — consistent with the lone
Glassdoor ~$135K SWE figure. Use this only as a sanity-check frame; it is not Arturo data.

**Confidence:** Headcount range = medium (multiple aggregators, but stale/estimated).
Compensation = low (one Glassdoor estimate; no verified bands). Anyone citing a precise
