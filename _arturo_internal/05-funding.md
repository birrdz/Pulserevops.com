# Arturo — Funding History (Internal Research Brief)

> Internal only — for Kory White's job prep. Facts only, no opinions. **Arturo is defunct** (ceased operations 2025 after an asset sale); all descriptions below are past tense. Amounts and dates are drawn from company press releases (Business Wire), investor sites, and aggregator profiles (Crunchbase, PitchBook, Tracxn). Where a figure is not confirmed by a primary source, it is marked "not publicly disclosed."

## Overview

Arturo, Inc. was a property-intelligence / property-analytics company built as a deep-learning spin-out from **American Family Insurance**. After roughly three years as an internal R&D effort, it was spun out as an independent company in 2018 and raised across a seed round (2019), a Series A (2020), and a Series B (2021). Aggregator profiles (Crunchbase, PitchBook) reported **total funding of approximately $47 million** across all rounds.

The publicly announced priced rounds — Series A ($8M) and Series B ($25M) — sum to $33M. The remaining ~$14M is attributable to the 2019 seed round and other early/undisclosed investment; the **exact seed amount was not publicly disclosed** in the press release. No priced round after the April 2021 Series B was ever publicly announced, and **no venture debt facility was publicly disclosed**.

## Round Detail

### Seed (May 2019)
Arturo announced the close of its seed series concurrent with its formal spin-out from American Family Insurance. American Family Insurance was named the lead investor and Arturo's co-developer. The dollar amount of the seed round was not publicly disclosed in the announcement.

### Series A — $8M (April 7, 2020)
The $8M Series A was **led by Crosslink Capital**, a San Francisco-based early-stage VC firm, with participation from **IAG Firemark Ventures**, the venture arm of IAG (Australia's largest general insurer). David Silverman (Managing Partner, Crosslink Capital) joined the board of directors; Scott Gunther (IAG Firemark Ventures) joined as a board observer. Proceeds were earmarked to advance the company's machine-learning and AI capabilities for the insurance market.

### Series B — $25M (April 27, 2021)
The $25M Series B was **led by Atlantic Bridge Capital**, with new investor **RPS Ventures** and existing investors **Crosslink Capital** and **IAG Firemark Ventures** participating. At announcement, Arturo reported that revenue had grown over 300% in the prior twelve months and that its client base included two of the top five U.S. P&C insurers plus the two leading insurers in the Australian market. Stated use of funds: expanding the technical team, refining the imagery-analytics technology, and establishing imagery-provider partnerships to extend coverage into Europe and Southeast Asia.

## Complete Investor Roster (across all rounds)

- **Crosslink Capital** — Series A lead; Series B participant
- **IAG Firemark Ventures** — Series A and Series B participant
- **Atlantic Bridge Capital** — Series B lead
- **RPS Ventures** — Series B participant
- **Plug and Play Tech Center** — listed among Arturo's investors on aggregator profiles (round/stage not specified in primary press releases)

Aggregators (Crunchbase) reference "8 investors" in total; the six named above are the ones corroborated by primary press releases and investor sites.

## Valuation & Later Rounds

- **Valuation:** Not publicly disclosed for any round (seed, Series A, or Series B). No primary source reported a pre- or post-money valuation.
- **Later rounds / debt:** No priced equity round after the April 2021 Series B was ever publicly announced, and **no venture debt facility was publicly disclosed**. Coverage from 2022 indicated the company anticipated raising again in late 2022 or early 2023, but no such round was publicly confirmed. The company subsequently ceased operations in 2025 following an asset sale.
