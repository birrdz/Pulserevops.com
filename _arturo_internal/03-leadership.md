# Arturo — Leadership & Team History

> Internal research brief for Kory White's job prep. Facts only. Arturo is **defunct** (ceased operations 2025) — all past tense.

## Summary

Arturo was a property-intelligence AI company spun out of American Family Insurance. Over its independent life (2018–2025) it cycled through **three CEOs**: co-founder John-Isaac "jC" Clark, then Greg Oslan, then Marty Smuin, who led the company through its 2025 asset sale and shutdown. The leadership turnover was concentrated in a roughly 12-month window (Sept 2022 – Sept/Oct 2023), and reporting explicitly framed it as a period of "personnel transitions."

## CEO succession (the three CEOs)

**1. John-Isaac "jC" Clark — co-founder / founding CEO (2018 – Sept 2022).**
Clark joined as CEO in August 2018 and led the formal spin-out of the product and team from American Family Insurance, which closed in 2019. Before Arturo he was Head of Commercial Product at DigitalGlobe (later Maxar), and earlier co-founded T-Sciences, building location analytics products around Google Earth/Maps — roughly a decade of geospatial background. Sources: [InsTech profile](https://www.instech.co/knowledge-centre/john-isaac-clark-ceo-arturo-property-insights-from-above/), [Forbes Business Council](https://www.forbes.com/sites/forbesbusinesscouncil/people/johnisaacclark/), [Carrier Management](https://www.carriermanagement.com/news/2019/07/29/195946.htm).

**2. Greg Oslan — CEO (Sept 1, 2022 – Sept/Oct 2023).**
Oslan was invited onto Arturo's board around May 2022 and appointed CEO effective September 1, 2022, succeeding Clark. His background included leadership roles at RiskSense, Narus, and U.S. government AI initiatives (DIU, JAIC). Asked directly whether Clark had been asked to step down, Oslan declined to comment, stating the company would "not comment on any of the past or any personnel transitions as there have been and will be more." Later reporting described his tenure as an **interim** CEO role of about one year; after Smuin took over, Oslan stayed on as an **advisor**. Sources: [Insurance Business America](https://www.insurancebusinessmag.com/us/news/technology/board-tasks-new-arturo-leadership-with-scaling-operations-422425.aspx), [BusinessWire Sept 1, 2022](https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20220901005170/en/Arturo-Welcomes-Additional-Leadership-as-it-Plans-for-Next-Phase-of-Industry-Growth).

**3. Marty Smuin — CEO (Sept 2023 – closure in 2025).**
Announced October 19, 2023 with an effective start in September 2023. Smuin had served on Arturo's board of directors for roughly two years before becoming CEO, and previously held COO / co-CEO roles at Weave Communications. He remained CEO through the company's 2025 asset sale and wind-down, and was the executive who publicly confirmed the closure. Sources: [BusinessWire Oct 19, 2023](https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20231019693849/en/Tech-Veteran-Marty-Smuin-Named-CEO-of-Arturo), [Insurance Business – "Arturo names new CEO"](https://www.insurancebusinessmag.com/us/news/technology/arturo-names-new-ceo-463997.aspx), [The Insurer, July 30, 2025](https://www.theinsurer.com/ti/news/arturo-ceases-operations-after-asset-sale-ceo-says-2025-07-30/).

Additional board names surfaced in secondary aggregator sources (e.g., Kevin Dillon of Atlantic Bridge, David Silverman of Crosslink Capital, and a Juan Pujadas board addition reportedly in May 2024), but these came from **low-reliability third-party profile sites and are not confirmed by primary sources** — treat as unverified.

## Leadership-instability signals (factual)

- Three CEOs in ~7 years, with two of the transitions inside a single ~13-month span (Sept 2022 → Sept/Oct 2023).
- The founding CEO (Clark) departed the top role in Sept 2022; the company declined to characterize the departure.
- Oslan's tenure was retrospectively described as "interim," and he shifted to an advisory role rather than remaining an executive.
- The final CEO (Smuin) was drawn from the existing board — an internal, board-driven succession rather than an outside search.
