# Arturo — US Operations & Market Position

> Internal research brief for Kory White's job prep. Facts only. **Arturo is DEFUNCT (ceased operations 2025)** — all past tense.

## Framing the original question: "Is Arturo expanding into the USA?"

The premise needs correcting. Arturo was **not** a foreign company moving into the US — it was **founded and headquartered in the United States from the start.** It was a deep-learning spin-out of **American Family Insurance** (a ~90-year-old carrier based in Madison, Wisconsin) and became independent in 2018 after roughly three years of internal R&D ([Coverager](https://coverager.com/arturo-announces-spin-out-from-american-family/); [InsurTech Digital](https://insurtechdigital.com/technology-and-ai/american-family-insurance-spin-out-arturo-innovates-proptech)).

The accurate arc is the reverse of "expanding into the USA": Arturo was **US-born**, expanded **internationally** (notably Australia), relocated its US HQ within the US (Denver → Utah, Nov 2024), and then **ceased operations in 2025** following an asset sale.

## US headquarters history

- **Original HQ: Denver, Colorado.** Arturo operated from Denver from its 2018 spin-out through 2024 ([CB Insights](https://www.cbinsights.com/company/arturo-1)).
- **Relocation to Utah (November 2024).** Arturo moved its headquarters to **South Jordan, Utah**, in the Salt Lake City metro area, with an additional office referenced in **Lehi, Utah** ([TechBuzz News](https://www.techbuzznews.com/arturo-an-innovative-property-insurance-imaging-ai-company-moves-to-salt-lake-city-2/); [Trending Insurance News](https://trendinginsurancenews.com/arturo-an-innovative-property-insurance-imaging-ai-company-moves-to-utah/)).
- **Stated rationale.** CEO Marty Smuin framed the move around Utah's tech sector ("Silicon Slopes"): *"Getting to Utah was the goal I had when I took over as CEO of Arturo in September of 2023,"* and *"I've loved watching the Utah tech sector grow"* ([TechBuzz News](https://www.techbuzznews.com/arturo-an-innovative-property-insurance-imaging-ai-company-moves-to-salt-lake-city-2/)). Smuin was based in Utah, and the relocation aligned the company with his location and the regional tech talent pool.

Note: this was an intra-US relocation (Colorado → Utah), not a market entry. American Family (Wisconsin) remained both a customer and an investor after the spin-out ([Coverager](https://coverager.com/arturo-announces-spin-out-from-american-family/)).

## US market focus and carrier clients

Arturo's core market was **US property & casualty (P&C) insurance**, alongside reinsurance, lending, and securities. It positioned itself for underwriting, pricing, portfolio risk, catastrophe response, and claims.

- By April 2021 the company reported traction among the **"top five P&C providers in the US and Australian markets"** and cited a **300%+ revenue increase** over the prior twelve months ([InsurTech Digital](https://insurtechdigital.com/technology-and-ai/american-family-insurance-spin-out-arturo-innovates-proptech)).
- **Named US clients/partners** across coverage include **American Family** (parent, customer, investor), **Hippo** (2019), and **USAA**.
- **LexisNexis Risk Solutions partnership (Oct 2020):** a US-focused alliance to deliver *"advanced analytics-powered roof solutions for U.S. home insurers"* ([PR Newswire](https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/lexisnexis-risk-solutions-and-arturo-team-up-to-deliver-the-next-generation-of-advanced-analytics-powered-roof-solutions-for-us-home-insurers-301158166.html); [LexisNexis Risk](https://risk.lexisnexis.com/about-us/press-room/press-release/20201022-arturo-alliance)).

## International expansion (for contrast with the "US expansion" question)

While US-headquartered, Arturo's most publicized deployment was **Australia**. Working with **Suncorp**, it processed roughly **9 million properties** (effectively every residential property in Australia) in about 48 hours. **IAG** was both a client and, via **IAG Firemark Ventures**, an investor. Management also signaled intent to expand in **European and APAC** markets ([InsurTech Digital](https://insurtechdigital.com/technology-and-ai/american-family-insurance-spin-out-arturo-innovates-proptech)). So the geographic expansion story ran **outward from the US**, not into it.

## Shutdown (2025) — the end of US operations

- **Ceased operations in 2025.** CEO Marty Smuin confirmed that Arturo had ceased operations following an **asset sale**, disclosed publicly around **July 30, 2025**; the company website went dark ([The Insurer](https://www.theinsurer.com/ti/news/arturo-ceases-operations-after-asset-sale-ceo-says-2025-07-30/); [Coverager](https://coverager.com/arturos-website-goes-dark-following-asset-sale/)).
- **Buyer.** Reporting describes the acquirer only as a **"strategic partner,"** with the transaction completing around **June 2025**. The **exact legal entity has not been publicly named** in available coverage. (Arturo's prior partnership with LexisNexis Risk Solutions has fueled speculation, but no source reviewed confirms the buyer's identity — treat any specific buyer name as unverified.)

## Net for Kory

Arturo was a **US company throughout its life** (American Family spin-out; HQ Denver → South Jordan, Utah). It served **US P&C carriers** as its home market, expanded **internationally to Australia**, and **shut down in 2025** after selling its assets. There is **no factual basis** for a present-tense "Arturo is expanding into the USA" claim — the company no longer operates.
