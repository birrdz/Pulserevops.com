# Arturo — Clients & Partnerships (internal brief)

> Facts only. Arturo ceased operations in 2025 following an asset sale — all relationships below are described in the past tense.

Arturo, the property-intelligence AI company spun out of American Family Insurance, sold its imagery-derived property analytics to a small roster of P&C insurers (reporting placed its customer count in the 10–20 range) and built at least one major data-provider alliance. Its named clients and partners included Hippo, Suncorp, IAG, USAA, and LexisNexis Risk Solutions.

## Hippo Insurance (US, July 2019)

Arturo's first widely publicized carrier relationship. In July 2019, Arturo — then described as incubated within American Family Insurance — announced that California insurtech Hippo had adopted its deep-learning property analytics. Arturo's models analyzed satellite, aerial, drone, and ground-level imagery to return near-real-time physical property characteristics that Hippo integrated into its proprietary policy-management system for pricing, underwriting, and renewals. Reported results: property detail returned in under five seconds, used to pre-fill application data such as roof material and pool presence, supporting Hippo's fast-quote model.
Sources: [Carrier Management](https://www.carriermanagement.com/news/2019/07/11/195322.htm), [Claims Journal](https://www.claimsjournal.com/news/national/2019/07/11/291904.htm), [Insurance Innovation Reporter](https://iireporter.com/hippo-adopts-arturo-property-analytics-to-optimize-underwriting-and-renewals/).

## IAG / IAG Firemark Ventures (Australia)

IAG, Australia's largest general insurer, was both an investor and a strategic partner. Its corporate venture arm, IAG Firemark Ventures (a $75M fund), invested in Arturo — reporting places the first investment in early 2020 — making IAG the first Arturo investor from outside the United States. IAG Firemark Ventures then participated again in Arturo's $25M Series B (announced April 2021, led by Atlantic Bridge Capital, with RPS Ventures and existing investors Crosslink Capital and IAG Firemark Ventures). IAG was also listed among Arturo's operating customers. The relationship gave Arturo a strategic foothold and imagery/analytics use case in the Australian market.
Sources: [IAG newsroom](https://www.iag.com.au/newsroom/innovation/iag-invests-arturo-ai-powered-analytics-start), [Business Wire — Series B](https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20210427005718/en/Arturo-Raises-%2425-Million-in-Series-B-Funding-for-AI-Platform-to-Help-PC-Insurers-and-Banks-Better-Understand-Physical-Properties), [Insurance News (AU)](https://www.insurancenews.com.au/corporate/iag-invests-in-us-artificial-intelligence-firm).

## Suncorp Group (Australia, October 2020)

Arturo's most quantified case study. Suncorp, a major Australian insurer and bank, selected Arturo to derive property characteristics across Australia from aerial imagery. Announced October 28, 2020, Arturo reported delivering 30-plus unique property characteristics across nearly 9 million records — roughly 85% of Australia's residential properties — in under 48 hours. For this engagement Arturo trained deep-learning models specifically for Australian imagery for the first time. Reported outcomes for Suncorp: attributes such as property size, distance to water, and presence of a pool or solar panels were derived without asking customers complex questions, which simplified the quote process, reduced quote drop-out, and removed the need to verify property attributes at claim time. Separate Australian trade coverage noted Suncorp used the imagery to roughly halve its customer disclosure list.
Sources: [Business Wire](https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20201028006203/en/Arturo-Delivers-Insights-Across-Nearly-9M-Australian-Properties), [Insurtech Insights](https://www.insurtechinsights.com/arturo-delivers-insights-across-nearly-9-million-australian-properties/), [insuranceNEWS.com.au](https://www.insurancenews.com.au/insurtech/suncorp-halves-disclosure-list-with-images-of-9-million-homes).

## LexisNexis Risk Solutions (US, October 2020)

Arturo's largest data-provider alliance, announced October 22, 2020. LexisNexis Risk Solutions and Arturo teamed to deliver next-generation analytics-powered roof solutions for U.S. home (and commercial property) insurers. The relationship combined LexisNexis's home/auto insurance claims data and geospatially analyzed weather-event data with Arturo's machine-learning analysis of aerial imagery. The stated logic: auto claims following hailstorms serve as leading indicators of area roof damage, and combining weather, auto/property claims, and imagery analytics produced a fuller view of roof condition than imagery alone. Arturo's imagery analytics enhanced the LexisNexis Rooftop / Roof Condition solution, used at new business and prior to renewal for underwriting decisions. Note for context: at Arturo's 2025 shutdown, reporting pointed to a "Risk Solutions" entity as the asset buyer — a possible tie to this prior LexisNexis Risk Solutions relationship (exact buyer entity remains to be verified separately).
Sources: [LexisNexis Risk Solutions press release](https://risk.lexisnexis.com/about-us/press-room/press-release/20201022-arturo-alliance), [PR Newswire](https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/lexisnexis-risk-solutions-and-arturo-team-up-to-deliver-the-next-generation-of-advanced-analytics-powered-roof-solutions-for-us-home-insurers-301158166.html), [Insurance Innovation Reporter](https://iireporter.com/lexisnexis-enhances-roof-solution-with-arturos-ai-analytics/).
