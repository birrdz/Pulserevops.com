# Arturo — Timeline & History (Founding → 2025 Shutdown)

> Internal research brief for Kory White's job prep. Facts only, cited inline. **Arturo is DEFUNCT** — all descriptions are past tense. Arturo was a property-intelligence AI company for the insurance ecosystem (arturo.ai).

---

## Chronological timeline

### Origins & spin-out (2015–2019)
- **~2015–2018 — Internal R&D at American Family Insurance.** Arturo grew out of more than three years of American Family Insurance research applying AI/deep learning to satellite, aerial, drone, and ground-level imagery to assess physical property characteristics. ([Business Wire, 2019-05-28](https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20190528005147/en/Arturo-Announces-Seed-Series-Close-and-Spin-out-from-American-Family-Insurance))
- **2018-08-06 — John-Isaac "JC" Clark joined as CEO** and began the formal process of spinning the Applied Machine Learning and Engineering teams out of American Family. Clark had a geospatial background (ex-DigitalGlobe). ([Business Wire, 2019-05-28](https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20190528005147/en/Arturo-Announces-Seed-Series-Close-and-Spin-out-from-American-Family-Insurance))
- **2019 (late April; announced 2019-05-28) — Spin-out formally completed** via the close of a Seed Series transaction, establishing Arturo as an independent company. American Family remained an investor. ([Business Wire](https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20190528005147/en/Arturo-Announces-Seed-Series-Close-and-Spin-out-from-American-Family-Insurance); [Coverager](https://coverager.com/arturo-announces-spin-out-from-american-family/))

### Funding & growth (2020–2022)
- **2020 (April) — Series A: $8M.** ([Coverager](https://coverager.com/property-analytics-startup-raises-25-million/))
- **2020-10-22 — LexisNexis Risk Solutions partnership** to deliver analytics-powered roof solutions for U.S. home insurers (combining LexisNexis claims/weather data with Arturo's aerial-imagery ML models). ([LexisNexis Risk Solutions press release](https://risk.lexisnexis.com/about-us/press-room/press-release/20201022-arturo-alliance); [Coverager](https://coverager.com/lexisnexis-and-arturo-partner-to-deliver-analytics-powered-roof-solutions/))

([Business Wire](https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20210427005718/en/Arturo-Raises-%2425-Million-in-Series-B-Funding-for-AI-Platform-to-Help-PC-Insurers-and-Banks-Better-Understand-Physical-Properties); [Carrier Management](https://www.carriermanagement.com/news/2021/04/27/219843.htm); [Atlantic Bridge](https://abven.com/news/atlantic-bridge-leads-25m-round-into-leading-ai-platform-arturo-to-help-p-c-insurers-and-banks-better-understand-physical-properties/))
- **2021-11-08 — Expanded relationship with Openly** (physical property characteristic data + predictive analysis). ([Business Wire](https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20211108005675/en/Arturo-Expands-Strategic-Relationship-with-Openly-Providing-Physical-Property-Characteristic-Data-and-Predictive-Analysis))
- **2022-09-08 — Vexcel Imaging strategic partnership**, naming Vexcel as a lead aerial-imagery provider. ([Business Wire](https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20220908005319/en/Arturo-Announces-Strategic-Partnership-with-Vexcel-Imaging))

### Leadership churn & rebrand (2022–2023)
- **~2022–2023 — Greg Oslan became CEO**, succeeding JC Clark (second CEO). ([Coverager: Arturo announces new CEO](https://coverager.com/arturo-announces-new-ceo/); [Crunchbase](https://www.crunchbase.com/person/greg-oslan))
- **2023-03-20 — New brand and product vision** positioning Arturo as property intelligence "for the entire insurance ecosystem." ([Business Wire](https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20231019693849/en/Tech-Veteran-Marty-Smuin-Named-CEO-of-Arturo); [Insurance Business](https://www.insurancebusinessmag.com/us/news/technology/arturo-names-new-ceo-463997.aspx))

### Product push, Utah move, and instability (2024)
- **2024-10-14 — Launched Change Detection technology** for property insurers. ([Business Wire](https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20241014874587/en/Arturo-Unveils-Groundbreaking-Change-Detection-Technology-for-Insurers); [Reinsurance News](https://www.reinsurancene.ws/property-insurers-to-benefit-from-arturos-newly-launched-change-detection-technology/))
- **2024-11 — Headquarters relocated from Denver, CO to South Jordan / Salt Lake City, Utah** ("Silicon Slopes"). Smuin said moving to Utah had been his goal since taking over as CEO in September 2023. ([TechBuzz News](https://www.techbuzznews.com/arturo-an-innovative-property-insurance-imaging-ai-company-moves-to-salt-lake-city-2/); [Trending Insurance News](https://trendinginsurancenews.com/arturo-an-innovative-property-insurance-imaging-ai-company-moves-to-utah/))
- **2024–2025 — Workforce instability.** Coverager reported significant layoffs and turnover (headcount fell to roughly 73 from ~95 a year earlier per LinkedIn figures cited), signaling strain ahead of the shutdown. ([Coverager: Instability at Arturo](https://coverager.com/instability-at-arturo/))

### Shutdown (2025)
- **~2025 mid-year (around June) — Asset sale completed.** In his late-July LinkedIn statement, CEO Marty Smuin said the asset sale had closed "a couple of months ago," placing completion in roughly mid-2025. ([Coverager: Arturo's website goes dark](https://coverager.com/arturos-website-goes-dark-following-asset-sale/))
- **2025 (mid-year) — Company website went dark** (arturo.ai taken offline), the first public signal of the wind-down. ([Coverager](https://coverager.com/arturos-website-goes-dark-following-asset-sale/))
- **2025-07-30 — Reported that Arturo had ceased operations following the asset sale**, per CEO Marty Smuin. Smuin stated the technology "will continue to thrive in the earth observation arena, delivering critical real-property data and insights to some of the largest insurance companies in the world." ([The Insurer, 2025-07-30](https://www.theinsurer.com/ti/news/arturo-ceases-operations-after-asset-sale-ceo-says-2025-07-30/); [Coverager](https://coverager.com/arturos-website-goes-dark-following-asset-sale/))

## What happened in the shutdown — and who bought the assets

Arturo did not raise a rescue round or file a conventional bankruptcy in the accessible reporting. Instead, it **sold its assets, wound down operations, and took its website offline** in mid-2025. The public sequence was: (1) website goes dark; (2) trade press (Coverager, The Insurer) confirms the asset sale and cessation of operations around July 30, 2025; (3) CEO Marty Smuin confirms via LinkedIn that the sale had closed "a couple of months" prior and that the underlying technology would continue under a new owner in the "earth observation arena." The layoffs and leadership churn through 2024–2025 (three CEOs in the company's life; ~23% workforce cut cited) were the visible run-up. ([Coverager: Instability](https://coverager.com/instability-at-arturo/); [Coverager: website goes dark](https://coverager.com/arturos-website-goes-dark-following-asset-sale/))

### Buyer — VERIFICATION FLAG (not confirmed)
**The exact legal entity that acquired Arturo's assets was NOT publicly named in the accessible reporting.** Coverager, The Insurer, and Smuin's own LinkedIn statement all described the acquirer only in general terms (a strategic partner active in "earth observation" / real-property data), without naming a company. ([The Insurer](https://www.theinsurer.com/ti/news/arturo-ceases-operations-after-asset-sale-ceo-says-2025-07-30/); [Coverager](https://coverager.com/arturos-website-goes-dark-following-asset-sale/))
