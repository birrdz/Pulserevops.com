# Best Mitsubishi Mirage Model Years (Ranked)

The **Mitsubishi Mirage** is one of the last truly cheap new cars sold in America, a subcompact built around low purchase price, **excellent fuel economy**, and bulletproof simplicity rather than power or refinement. The modern hatchback and **Mirage G4 sedan** arrived for the 2014 model year in North America, powered by a frugal **1.2-liter three-cylinder** engine paired with a **CVT** or five-speed manual. A 2017 refresh sharpened the styling, added equipment, and improved noise isolation, while later years gained standard safety features. The Mirage was discontinued after the 2024 model year. This ranking covers the best Mirage model years, their powertrains, the few known issues, and where the value lies on the used market today.

## Direct Answer

The **best overall Mitsubishi Mirage is the 2021-2024 facelifted model**, which combines the highest level of standard safety equipment, the best build quality of the run, slightly more sound deadening, and the longest remaining factory powertrain warranty, all while keeping the signature 36-43 mpg efficiency. For shoppers focused purely on value, the **best value is the 2017-2018 refreshed hatchback**, which brought updated styling, a better interior, and added features at used prices that are now extremely low. Avoid the rough, less-equipped 2014-2015 first North American years unless the price is exceptionally cheap, and always favor a documented maintenance history given how these economy cars are often used hard.

## 1. 2021-2024 Facelifted Mirage (Hatch & G4) 🏆 BEST OVERALL

@@PRODUCT name="Mitsubishi Mirage (2021-2024 facelift)" site="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mitsubishi_Mirage"

The final-generation facelifted Mirage is the nameplate's high point. The 2021 update brought revised front-end styling, new wheels, and the most complete safety roster of any Mirage, with **forced-fed standard features like forward-collision mitigation and lane-departure warning** on most trims. The **1.2-liter three-cylinder** (78 hp) carries over with a **CVT** or five-speed manual, still returning an outstanding **36 city / 43 highway mpg**.

These are the **newest, lowest-mileage** Mirages available and carry the **longest remaining 10-year/100,000-mile powertrain warranty**, Mitsubishi's strongest ownership argument. Build quality and noise isolation are the best of the run. If your budget reaches a recent used example, this is the Mirage to buy.

## 2. 2017-2018 Refreshed Hatchback 💎 BEST VALUE

@@PRODUCT name="Mitsubishi Mirage (2017-2018 refresh)" site="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mitsubishi_Mirage"

The 2017 mid-cycle refresh is the value champion. It brought **updated front and rear styling**, a reworked interior with better materials, added sound deadening, and a retuned suspension for a more composed ride. The **1.2-liter three-cylinder** gained a small bump to **78 hp**, and standard equipment grew to include features like a touchscreen and **standard Bluetooth**.

**The best value is a 2017-2018 ES or SE hatchback**, which bundles a rearview camera, alloy wheels, and keyless entry at a used price that now sits among the cheapest reliable cars you can buy. With the same **40+ mpg highway** efficiency and Mitsubishi's long powertrain warranty, this refreshed Mirage offers the most car for the least money.

## 3. 2017-2020 Mirage G4 Sedan

@@PRODUCT name="Mitsubishi Mirage G4 sedan (2017-2020)" site="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mitsubishi_Mirage"

The **Mirage G4** is the four-door sedan version, offering a real trunk and a slightly longer wheelbase for marginally better ride quality. It shares the **1.2-liter three-cylinder** and **CVT** with the hatchback and returns nearly identical efficiency, around **35 city / 41 highway mpg**.

The G4 appeals to buyers who want a **conventional sedan shape** and a lockable trunk rather than a hatch. It is just as simple and inexpensive to own, and the refreshed 2017-and-later cars benefit from the same interior and feature upgrades. For commuters who prefer a sedan and prioritize running costs, a clean used G4 is a sensible, low-risk pick.

## 4. 2019-2020 Hatchback

@@PRODUCT name="Mitsubishi Mirage hatchback (2019-2020)" site="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mitsubishi_Mirage"

The 2019-2020 hatchback carries the refreshed styling and adds gradual equipment improvements, including expanded availability of **Apple CarPlay and Android Auto** on the touchscreen-equipped trims. Mechanically it is unchanged, with the proven **1.2-liter three-cylinder** and **CVT** delivering the familiar **36/43 mpg** rating.

These cars split the difference between the value-priced 2017-2018 models and the safety-rich 2021-2024 facelift. They are newer than the early refresh cars, often with lower mileage, while still costing meaningfully less than the final model years. A well-kept 2019-2020 hatchback in **ES or SE trim** is a strong middle-ground choice for a budget commuter.

## 5. 2021-2024 Mirage G4 Sedan

@@PRODUCT name="Mitsubishi Mirage G4 sedan (2021-2024)" site="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mitsubishi_Mirage"

The facelifted **Mirage G4 sedan** brings the same updated front-end styling and expanded standard safety features as the final hatchback, including **forward-collision mitigation** on most trims. It keeps the **1.2-liter three-cylinder**, **CVT**, and roughly **35/41 mpg** efficiency, plus the practical sedan trunk.

This is the newest sedan option and carries the **longest remaining powertrain warranty**, making it the safest used G4 buy from a coverage standpoint. Buyers who specifically want a small, affordable sedan with modern driver-assistance features and Mitsubishi's warranty should target a recent G4. It is slightly less common than the hatch, so shop patiently for the right trim and condition.

## 6. 2016 Hatchback (Pre-Refresh, Late)

@@PRODUCT name="Mitsubishi Mirage hatchback (2016)" site="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mitsubishi_Mirage"

The 2016 hatchback is the last of the pre-refresh North American cars, sold before the 2017 styling and interior update. It uses the original **1.2-liter three-cylinder** (74 hp in this period) with the **CVT** or five-speed manual and delivers strong efficiency in the high-30s to low-40s mpg.

These cars are **noisier and more basic** than the refreshed models, with simpler interiors and fewer standard features. The upside is a **very low used price**. For a buyer who wants the cheapest possible reliable transportation and does not mind a no-frills cabin, a clean 2016 Mirage is serviceable. Just confirm the CVT shifts smoothly and the service history is documented.

## 7. 2014-2015 First North American Years

@@PRODUCT name="Mitsubishi Mirage (2014-2015)" site="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mitsubishi_Mirage"

The 2014-2015 hatchback marked the Mirage's modern return to the U.S. market. These early cars are the **most basic and least refined** of the run, with the original **1.2-liter three-cylinder** (74 hp), a coarse engine note, and noticeable road and wind noise. Equipment is minimal on base trims.

The strong points are **class-leading fuel economy** (around 37/44 mpg) and an exceptionally **low purchase price**, both new and now used. Reliability is generally solid thanks to the simple drivetrain. Treat these as ultra-budget commuters rather than comfortable cars. Inspect the CVT, check for accident history given the lightweight body, and prioritize examples with full maintenance records.

## 8. Mirage Manual Transmission Variants

@@PRODUCT name="Mitsubishi Mirage 5-speed manual" site="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mitsubishi_Mirage"

The **five-speed manual** Mirage is a distinct variant worth seeking for the right buyer. It avoids the **CVT** entirely, which appeals to drivers who prefer a clutch pedal and want to eliminate any concern about long-term CVT wear. The manual also makes the modest **1.2-liter three-cylinder** feel slightly more engaging.

Manual Mirages are **less common** and usually found in base ES trims, so options are limited. They typically cost a little less than CVT examples and can be more enjoyable to drive despite the low power. For enthusiasts of simple, lightweight cars or shoppers who simply trust a manual over an automatic, a manual Mirage is a charming, dependable budget choice.

## 9. Mirage Special and Anniversary Editions

@@PRODUCT name="Mitsubishi Mirage special edition trims" site="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mitsubishi_Mirage"

Over the years Mitsubishi released **appearance-focused special editions** of the Mirage, such as the **Limited Edition** and other dealer or anniversary packages that bundled unique paint, badging, and added equipment at a small premium. Mechanically these are identical to standard Mirages, sharing the **1.2-liter three-cylinder** and **CVT**.

The appeal is purely in the **added features and distinct trim** rather than performance. For a buyer who wants a slightly better-equipped Mirage with some visual flair, a clean special edition can be a smart find if priced near a standard model. Confirm exactly which features the package included, as content varied by year and market.

## 10. High-Mileage Budget Mirages (Any Year)

@@PRODUCT name="Mitsubishi Mirage high-mileage examples" site="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mitsubishi_Mirage"

At the bottom of the ranking are **high-mileage Mirages** of any year, the cheapest entry into the model. The simple **1.2-liter three-cylinder** is known for durability, and many examples soldier past 150,000 miles with basic maintenance, so a high-mileage car is not automatically a bad bet.

The cautions are real, however. Inspect the **CVT** carefully for shudder or hesitation, check for **suspension and bushing wear** common on light economy cars, and verify the timing chain and cooling system have been maintained. **Documented service history is essential.** For absolute lowest-cost transportation, a well-kept high-mileage Mirage works, but buy with eyes open and budget for deferred maintenance.

## What to Watch For When Buying

The most important step when buying a used Mirage is to **inspect the CVT** on automatic cars, since the continuously variable transmission is the component most likely to show wear; test for shudder, hesitation, or a flaring sensation under acceleration. Confirm the **CVT fluid has been serviced** at the recommended intervals, as neglected fluid is the leading cause of trouble.

Beyond the transmission, the **1.2-liter three-cylinder is generally durable**, but verify the timing chain, cooling system, and oil-change history. Because Mirages are light, inexpensive cars often used as commuters or first cars, **check carefully for accident damage and curb wear**, inspect tires and brakes, and confirm all electronics work. Mitsubishi's **10-year/100,000-mile powertrain warranty** may still apply to newer examples, so verify remaining coverage. Documented maintenance records outweigh a rock-bottom price every time.

## How to Choose

Match the Mirage to your priorities. For the **best blend of safety, condition, and warranty coverage**, the 2021-2024 facelifted hatch or G4 is the answer, with the most standard driver-assistance features and the longest remaining factory protection. For the **best value with solid features**, a 2017-2018 refreshed hatchback is hard to beat at today's used prices.

Buyers who want a **sedan body and a trunk** should target a Mirage G4, while drivers who prefer a clutch or distrust automatics should seek a **five-speed manual** example. Bargain hunters can consider 2014-2016 cars or high-mileage examples, but should always favor documented service and a clean CVT. In every case, prioritize maintenance history over the lowest sticker price.

## FAQ

**Which Mitsubishi Mirage years should I avoid?**
There are no catastrophic years to avoid outright, but the **2014-2015 first North American models** are the most basic and least refined, with more noise and fewer features. Buy those only at a very low price, and on any automatic car, verify the CVT has been properly serviced.

**Is the Mitsubishi Mirage reliable?**
Yes, for an ultra-budget car. The **simple 1.2-liter three-cylinder** is durable and the drivetrain is uncomplicated, which is why many Mirages exceed 150,000 miles. The main long-term wear item is the CVT, so prioritize examples with documented transmission fluid service.

**What kind of fuel economy does the Mirage get?**
Excellent. Most automatic hatchbacks are rated around **36 city / 43 highway mpg**, among the best for any non-hybrid gasoline car. The G4 sedan is slightly lower at roughly 35/41 mpg. Real-world highway efficiency in the 40s is achievable.

**Should I buy the hatchback or the G4 sedan?**
Choose the **hatchback** for maximum cargo flexibility and the lowest price, or the **G4 sedan** if you prefer a conventional trunk and a slightly smoother ride. Both share the same engine, CVT, efficiency, and warranty, so the decision comes down to body style preference.

## Bottom Line

The Mitsubishi Mirage is one of the cheapest, most fuel-efficient used cars available, built for simplicity rather than refinement. The **2021-2024 facelifted models are the best overall pick**, offering the most safety features, the best build quality, and the longest remaining warranty, while the **2017-2018 refreshed hatchback delivers the best value**. On any automatic car, **inspect and verify CVT service** above all else, and always favor a documented maintenance history. Bought carefully, the Mirage delivers dependable, ultra-economical transportation at a price almost nothing else matches.
