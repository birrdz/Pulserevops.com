How do reef tanks keep aquarium water clear and stable?

Direct Answer

Clear water starts with filtration, light, and steady parameters.

Why it matters

Murky water usually means dirt in motion, not mystery chemistry. Fix flow and export sludge and clarity returns.

Body image 1

Deep Dive

Good flow and a clean filter keep particles moving out of the water column.

Keep it stable

Hold salinity, alkalinity, and light on a schedule. Small daily habits beat big weekend rescue jobs.
