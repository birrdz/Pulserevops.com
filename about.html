About · Who we are

25 years of revenue-operations opinions — in one place.

PULSE is the working opinion library of Kory White, a Chief Revenue Officer with 25 years in the field. These are my takes — formed in real deals, real teams, and real board rooms — on the questions operators actually ask. Free to read. No paywall, no signup.

In one line: a collection of one CRO's hard-won opinions, gathered over 25 years and written down so you don't have to learn them the expensive way.

What this is

This isn't a neutral encyclopedia, and it doesn't pretend to be. Most "answers" online are anonymous, hedged, and written to rank. Mine are the opposite: opinionated, specific, and signed. I take a question operators actually ask and tell you what I think and what I'd do — drawn from 25 years of actually doing it, not a summary of what's already out there. Read it the way you'd read a seasoned operator's column, not a textbook.

It's built as a destination, not a funnel. The opinions are the product.

Who's behind it

Every word here reflects the judgment of Kory White, a Chief Revenue Officer with 25 years architecting revenue operations, sales teams, and go-to-market motions — roughly $3B in revenue scaled across SaaS, services, and B2B. I set the standard for every entry and I stand behind every opinion in this library. Based in Stevensville, Maryland.

Reach me on LinkedIn, see my work on The Executive Review, or schedule a call.
