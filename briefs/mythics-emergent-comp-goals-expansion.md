### General deep dive → Mythics Emergent tailored ideas

## Part 1 — How to set goals for sales reps

### 1.1 Start from the economics, not the stretch slogan

Good rep goals are **derived**, not dreamed. Work backward:

1. **Company plan** — revenue, gross margin, bookings, cash, mix (new vs renew vs expand)
2. **Capacity** — how many full-time sellers, ramps, open seats, coverage model
3. **Attainable territory potential** — what can this book of business *realistically* produce in 12 months?
4. **Individual quota** — territory potential × coverage × historical conversion, then stress-test

If quota > credible territory potential, you don’t have a motivation problem — you have a **design** problem. Reps will sandbag, churn, or game the plan.

### 1.2 Separate *outcome goals* from *activity goals*

| Layer | Examples | Use |
|--------|----------|-----|
| **Outcomes** | Quota (bookings / revenue / margin $), new logos, expansion $, multi-year ACV | Comp primary |
| **Leading indicators** | Qualified pipeline $, stage conversion, cycle time, multi-thread rate | Coaching / SPIFs, rarely core OTE |
| **Hygiene** | CRM hygiene, forecast accuracy, mutual action plans | Management, not big $ |

Pay mostly on **outcomes you can audit**. Use leading indicators for **manager 1:1s** and short SPIFs — not as 40% of OTE, or you’ll pay for busywork.

### 1.3 Goal quality checklist (nuance)

- **Credible**: P50 territory math supports it; P90 is upside, not the plan
- **Controllable**: Don’t hang quota on partner MDF or a single megadeal the rep can’t move
- **Clear time box**: Annual with quarterly checkpoints beats vague “crush it”
- **Mix-aware**: New logo vs renew vs expansion have different difficulty — weight or split quotas
- **Ramp-aware**: Months 1–3 / 4–6 / 7–12 curves; don’t full-quota a month-2 hire
- **Team vs individual**: Overlay / SE / channel need **rules of engagement** before goals go live
- **Forecast ≠ quota**: Forecast is a prediction; quota is a contract. Don’t confuse them in 1:1s

### 1.4 Goal-setting conversation (what good managers actually do)

1. Show the **territory model** (accounts, whitespace, pipeline, historical close rates)
2. Agree **what “good” looks like** at 70 / 100 / 120%
3. Name the **2–3 plays** that will move the number (not 17 initiatives)
4. Write down **dependencies** (SE capacity, deal desk, contract vehicles, legal SLA)
5. Revisit at 30/60/90 — adjust territory or support, **not** the integrity of the plan mid-year unless the market broke

## Part 2 — How to set up a compensation plan

### 2.1 Design sequence (do this order)

1. **Role architecture** — hunter, farmer, overlay, channel, inside, enterprise AE
2. **Pay mix** — base / variable (e.g. 50/50, 60/40, 70/30)
3. **On-target earnings (OTE)** — market + scarcity + complexity
4. **Primary measure** — what one number (or tight set) drives variable
5. **Accelerators / decelerators** — above and below quota behavior
6. **Credit & splits** — who gets paid when multiple people touch the deal
7. **Governance** — SPIF calendar, exception process, clawbacks, plan doc + examples
8. **Model it** — 10 fictional reps at 50/80/100/120/150% — check cost of sales and fairness
9. **Communicate** — one-pager + worked examples + “what if” FAQ
10. **Freeze** — mid-year plan changes destroy trust; change annually unless catastrophic

### 2.2 Pay mix by motion (typical ranges)

| Motion | Base / Variable | Why |
|--------|-----------------|-----|
| Outbound new logo / hunter | 40/60 – 50/50 | High leverage on hustle |
| Enterprise complex / public sector | 50/50 – 60/40 | Long cycles, more process |
| Account management / renewals | 60/40 – 70/30 | Retention + expansion balance |
| Overlay / SE-influenced | Higher base | Less sole control of close |

### 2.3 Non-negotiable plan hygiene

- **Written plan** with definitions: bookings vs revenue vs margin, when credit dates, what counts
- **No silent caps** (or be explicit if you cap)
- **Clawbacks** for cancelled deals / unpaid invoices — state timing (e.g. 90 days)
- **Draws** for ramps — recoverable vs non-recoverable, stated clearly
- **SPIF budget** as a % of variable (e.g. ≤10%) so the core plan stays sacred
- **Deal desk alignment** — if finance won’t recognize it, don’t put it in comp

## Part 3 — Typical / traditional comp plans

### 3.1 Classic AE (still the workhorse)

- **50/50 or 60/40** OTE  
- **Commission rate** = variable ÷ quota (linear to 100%)  
- **Accelerator** 1.2×–2.0× after 100%  
- **Threshold** sometimes at 50–70% (no pay until then) — use carefully; demotivates early year  
- **Paid on bookings** or invoice; public sector often **bookings on award / PO** with clawback risk

**Pros:** Simple, easy to explain, market-standard.  
**Cons:** Encourages sandbagging, year-end stuffing, neglect of hard strategic accounts if only $ matters.

### 3.2 Land-and-expand (split metrics)

- **New logo quota** + **expansion quota** (separate or weighted)  
- Or **first-year ACV** vs **NARR / net retention** for AM roles  

**Pros:** Stops “logo dumping” that never expands.  
**Cons:** More complex credit; needs clean CRM product hierarchy.

### 3.3 Margin-weighted or “quality revenue”

- Pay on **gross margin $** or apply **multipliers** (cloud vs license vs services)  
- Or **haircut** low-margin / high-discount deals  

**Pros:** Aligns to company economics.  
**Cons:** Reps need visibility into margin; finance must publish deal scores fast.

### 3.4 Team / pod overlay

- Pod of AE + SE + CSM shares a **team component** (10–20% of variable)  
- Rest individual  

**Pros:** Rewards collaboration on complex deals.  
**Cons:** Free-rider risk; needs clear pod membership.

### 3.5 Channel / partner-influenced

- Direct vs partner-sourced rates differ  
- **Registration** rules to prevent channel conflict  

**Pros:** Matches how revenue actually arrives.  
**Cons:** Politics; must be airtight.

## Part 4 — Progressive & experimental plans

Use these when the classic plan **optimizes the wrong behavior**. Still keep 70%+ of variable on a clean primary metric.

### 4.1 Multi-year / ARR durability bonuses

- Kicker for **2–3 year** terms, or paid uplift when renewal risk is low  
- **Idea:** 100% credit at sign + retention bonus at anniversary  

Useful when one-year deals create churn theater.

### 4.2 “Customer outcomes” or value SPIFs (narrow)

- Short SPIF tied to **go-live**, adoption milestone, or certified use-case — *not* soft NPS as core OTE  
- Works when implementation quality drives expansion  

### 4.3 Territory equity / “book value” thinking

- Treat territory like a **portfolio**: pay for growing account equity (penetration score), not only this quarter’s PO  
- Experimental: annual **whitespace capture** bonus for named strategic accounts  

### 4.4 Drawing rights / uncapped with guardrails

- Uncapped accelerators + **deal review** above a size threshold  
- Prevents “lottery ticket” toxicity while keeping upside alive  

### 4.5 Skills / credential gates (public sector friendly)

- Accelerator unlocks only if rep maintains **certifications / clearances / vehicle fluency**  
- Aligns enablement with earnings — common in partner/reseller cultures  

### 4.6 Pod P&L or “mini-GM” experiments

- Senior pods measured on **contribution margin** of their book  
- Rare; needs mature finance ops  

### 4.7 What to avoid (even if trendy)

- Paying big % on **activity points** long-term  
- **Stacking** 6 SPIFs that dwarf the core plan  
- Changing measures every quarter (“flavor of the month”)  
- MBOs that can’t be audited

## Part 5 — Expansion & new territory expansion

### 5.1 Types of expansion (don’t mix them in one quota blindly)

1. **Account expansion** — more wallet in existing logos (upsell, cross-sell, seats, modules)  
2. **Geographic / segment expansion** — new states, agencies, civilian vs DoD, SLED vs Fed  
3. **Motion expansion** — adding services/cloud attach to a license-heavy book  
4. **Capacity expansion** — more reps on the same model  

Each needs different **goals, comp, and ramp**.

### 5.2 Account expansion (farming done right)

- **Installed base map**: products, renew dates, competitors, political map (economic buyer vs technical)  
- **Expansion quota** separate or weighted so renewals don’t hide laziness  
- **Credit rules** when hunter lands and farmer expands (12-month handoff is common)  
- **Success dependency**: if attach requires delivery, don’t 100% commission AE for “sold” that SE can’t staff  

### 5.3 New territory / greenfield

**Before assigning quota:**

- Total addressable accounts in ICP  
- Access reality (vehicles, past performance, relationships, set-asides)  
- Sales cycle length distribution  
- Competitive density  

**Ramp pattern (example):**

| Period | Expectation |
|--------|-------------|
| 0–3 mo | Pipeline build, meetings, vehicle fluency — activity + milestone SPIFs |
| 4–6 mo | First meaningful pipeline; partial quota |
| 7–12 mo | Full quota or 80→100% ramp |

**Coverage models:**

- **Named accounts** (best for enterprise / agency)  
- **Geographic** (risky if agencies don’t cluster cleanly)  
- **Segment pods** (Fed civilian / DoD / SLED / Edu)  

### 5.4 Expansion without blowing up culture

- Publish **rules of engagement** *before* opening a territory (who owns whom)  
- **Sunset** hunting rights on accounts after win + N months  
- Comp **splits** for overlays (cloud specialist, cyber, services) agreed in writing  
- When opening a new region: **seed with 2–3 lighthouse pursuits**, not 400 cold logos and a full quota  

### 5.5 Leading indicators for expansion health

- % of revenue from top 10 accounts (concentration risk)  
- Expansion ACV / new logo ACV ratio  
- Multi-product attach rate  
- Cycle time by segment  
- Win rate on competitive displacements vs install-base

## Part 6 — Mythics Emergent Group — tailored plan & ideas

*After the general framework: how this maps to a public-sector IT solutions partner (VAR / distributor / SI) spanning Oracle, Red Hat, Adobe, cloud, hardware, and services — federal + SLED — with long cycles, contract vehicles, and multi-threaded pursuits.*

### 6.1 What makes MEG different (design constraints)

- **Long, procedural cycles** — RFPs, GWACs (e.g. SEWP, CIO-CS), option years, protests → pay mix should **not** be pure hunter 40/60 for most enterprise roles  
- **Vehicle & partner fluency is skill capital** — certifications and vehicle literacy deserve **enablement gates / accelerators**, not just “sell more”  
- **Mix matters** — license, cloud, hardware, support, professional services have different margin and effort → **margin or mix multipliers** beat raw bookings-only if finance can score deals fast  
- **Land often needs services to expand** — split **product bookings** vs **services attach** or you’ll starve delivery  
- **Hunter / farmer / overlay** must be explicit across Mythics vs Emergent motions and specialist overlays  
- **PE-backed growth era** — plans should reward **durable growth** (multi-year, expand in-account) not only year-end stuffing  

### 6.2 Suggested role × goal × pay mix (starting point)

| Role | Primary goals | Pay mix (start) | Notes |
|------|---------------|-----------------|-------|
| **Enterprise AE (Fed / SLED)** | Bookings + strategic logo / vehicle pursuits | 55/45 or 60/40 | Threshold gentle; accelerator on multi-year & cloud attach |
| **Hunter / new logo** | New agencies / accounts | 50/50 | Named greenfield list; ramp 2–3 quarters |
| **Account / expansion AE** | Expansion $ + renewal quality | 60/40 or 65/35 | Separate expansion quota; retention guardrail |
| **Overlay (cloud / cyber / apps)** | Influenced revenue + attach rate | 60/40–70/30 | Clear split tables with AE |
| **Inside / renewals** | Renew + small expand | 70/30 | Speed + accuracy |

### 6.3 MEG-shaped goal design

1. **Quota from territory math** — named agencies + renew calendar + whitespace by stack (Oracle / RH / Adobe / services), not a flat national stretch number  
2. **Dual scorecard for complex AEs**  
   - 70–80% **bookings / margin $**  
   - 20–30% **expansion or multi-product attach** (auditable)  
3. **Vehicle fluency milestone (ramp)** — e.g. demonstrated pursuit on approved vehicle before full variable unlock (coaching + small SPIF, not gotcha)  
4. **Lighthouse pursuits** — 2–4 named annual “must advance” opportunities with **stage-exit coaching**, not fake activity comp  

### 6.4 MEG-shaped comp mechanics (practical)

- **Core:** linear to 100% on primary measure; **1.3–1.7× accelerator** above 100%  
- **Mix multipliers (example ideas):**  
  - Cloud / subscription ≥ 1.1–1.25×  
  - Strategic services attach ≥ 1.15×  
  - Deep-discount / low-margin ≤ 0.7–0.85×  
- **Multi-year kicker:** +X% credit or bonus pool for ≥2-year commitments  
- **Clawbacks:** align to public-sector award reality (protest windows, funding) — write them down  
- **SPIFs (≤10% of variable budget):** certification completion, first cloud win in a named account, clean forecast accuracy streak  

### 6.5 Expansion & new territory ideas for MEG

**Account expansion**

- Build **account penetration scorecards** (which Oracle/RH/Adobe pillars present vs whitespace)  
- Expansion AE / farmer owns **year-2+** wallet; hunter gets defined handoff + trailing credit (e.g. 6–12 months)  
- Tie a slice of variable to **attach** (cloud or services) on install base — small but visible  

**New territory / segment**

- Open by **segment pod** (e.g. specific civilian agencies, state cluster, edu) with a **seed pursuit list**, not raw geography  
- Ramp: Q1 pipeline & meetings → Q2 first awards → Q3–Q4 full quota  
- Pair every new territory AE with **overlay + capture support** for first 2 pursuits (capacity is the real constraint)  
- Rules of engagement across Mythics / Emergent / overlays published **before** the pod goes live  

### 6.6 12-month “tailor plan” sketch (ideas, not a signed plan)

| Quarter | Focus | Comp / goal emphasis |
|---------|--------|----------------------|
| **Q1** | Clean territories, ROE, scorecards, ramp draws | Hygiene SPIFs; partial quota |
| **Q2** | Pipeline quality + 2 lighthouse advances | Primary commissions live; attach SPIF |
| **Q3** | Expansion plays on top accounts | Expansion weight visible in dashboards |
| **Q4** | Multi-year & mix quality (not just volume) | Accelerators + multi-year kicker; no junk SPIFs |

**Governance:** one annual plan doc, monthly deal-desk examples, quarterly business review on *plan health* (not constant plan rewrites).

### 6.7 Metrics MEG leadership should watch (beyond quota hit)

- Bookings mix: license / cloud / hardware / services  
- Net expansion in strategic accounts  
- Win rate by vehicle  
- Average sales cycle and slip rate  
- Cost of sales vs contribution margin  
- Rep retention in first 18 months (plan fairness signal)  

---

## Closing

**General rule:** goals from territory economics → pay on few auditable outcomes → accelerators for the *quality* of growth you want → expansion treated as its own design problem.

**For Mythics Emergent:** bias toward **slightly higher base**, **mix- and multi-year-aware variable**, **explicit hunter/farmer/overlay splits**, and **territory pods seeded with real pursuits** — because public-sector IT partnership rewards craft, patience, and attach, not just raw activity.
