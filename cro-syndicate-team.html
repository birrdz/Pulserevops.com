CRO Syndicate Partnership FAQ

What is CRO Syndicate?

CRO Syndicate is a global sales and services boutique advisory firm that places world-class fractional and interim Chief Revenue Officers — its network of “Practitioner CROs” — with ambitious companies on a fractional, interim, project, and coaching basis. Founded by Lisa Pettigrew, its roster has produced breakthrough results for Fortune 500 companies and driven billions in global revenue.

What is a Practitioner CRO?

A Practitioner CRO is an operator who has actually run the revenue motion — built teams, owned forecasts, carried the number — rather than a career consultant who only advises. They integrate with your team and culture and share accountability for driving results.

How does CRO Syndicate engage with companies?

On a fractional, interim, project, or coaching basis — embedded revenue leadership sized to what the company needs. Engagements range from leading the revenue org day to day, to bridging a vacant CRO seat, to coaching a founder or an existing leader.
