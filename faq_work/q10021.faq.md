## FAQ

**Why do vendors get mutual action plans wrong for inbound SDR teams on HubSpot?**
Vendor content treats mutual action plans as a template download, not as tracked CRM data, so the plans get ignored after the first call. On HubSpot the plan has to live as fields and tasks on the deal.

**Which HubSpot fields prove a mutual action plan is alive?**
Define 3-5 proof fields such as MAP-created date, next mutual milestone, milestone owner, and last MAP-update date. Done is clean data on those fields, not activity counts.

**How does inbound SDR flow affect mutual action plans?**
Inbound SDRs hand off fast, so the MAP often never gets created before the AE meeting. Setting the MAP fields at handoff in HubSpot prevents the plan from being skipped.

**Who owns mutual action plan adoption?**
A single RevOps DRI owns the fields and rollback, so every inbound deal records its plan the same way.

**How do you confirm MAPs are no longer ignored?**
Report one weekly Pulse metric, the share of active deals with a current MAP milestone, in leadership reviews with no shadow spreadsheets.
