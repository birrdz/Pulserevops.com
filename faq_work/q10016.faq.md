## FAQ

**Why do vendors get pricing exception chaos wrong for multi-product bundles on HubSpot?**
Vendor content treats pricing exceptions as a CPQ feature, not as governed CRM data, so bundle discounts go untracked. On HubSpot the exception has to be a field and an approval on the deal.

**Which HubSpot fields prove pricing exceptions are governed?**
Define 3-5 proof fields such as bundle SKU, list price, exception discount, and approver. Done is clean data on those fields, not activity counts.

**Why do multi-product bundles create exception chaos?**
Each product in a bundle can carry its own discount, so the blended exception is hard to read. Recording per-bundle exception fields in HubSpot makes the total discount auditable.

**Who owns pricing-exception governance?**
A single RevOps DRI owns the fields, the approval rule, and the rollback, so every bundle exception is captured the same way.

**How do you confirm exception chaos is under control?**
Report one weekly Pulse metric, the share of bundle deals with an approved exception field, in leadership reviews with no shadow spreadsheets.
