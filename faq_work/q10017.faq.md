## FAQ

**What CRM fields prove you fixed stage inflation in Zoho CRM?**
Define 3-5 proof fields such as stage entered date, stage exit criteria met, deal owner, and stage-downgrade flag. Done means clean data on those fields, not logged activity.

**Why does stage inflation appear after a Zoho CRM migration?**
Migrations often lose the exit-criteria fields that gated each stage, so reps advance deals without proof. Re-create those gating fields natively in Zoho before adding any tool.

**How does land-and-expand motion drive stage inflation?**
Land-and-expand deals reopen and re-stage often, so reps inflate stages to show momentum on expansion. An exit-criteria field per stage in Zoho holds those deals honest.

**Who owns stage definitions?**
A single RevOps DRI owns the exit criteria and rollback, so every land-and-expand deal is staged the same way.

**How do you confirm inflation is dropping?**
Report one weekly Pulse metric, the rate of stage downgrades after review, in leadership reviews, sourced from Zoho with no shadow spreadsheets.
