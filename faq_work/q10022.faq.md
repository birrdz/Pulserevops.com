## FAQ

**What CRM fields prove you fixed MQL decay in Zoho CRM?**
Define 3-5 proof fields such as MQL date, MQL-to-touch latency, follow-up owner, and decay-flag date. Done means clean data on those fields, not logged activity.

**Why does MQL decay show up after a Zoho CRM migration?**
Migrations often drop the timestamp fields that measured how fast MQLs were worked, so decay becomes invisible. Re-create those fields natively in Zoho before adding any tool.

**How does outbound SDR work change MQL decay?**
For outbound SDRs, MQLs compete with cold sequences, so inbound MQLs age out. A latency field forces the SDR queue to surface decaying MQLs in Zoho.

**Who owns the MQL-decay fields?**
A single RevOps DRI owns the field design and rollback, so MQL freshness is measured one way across every SDR.

**How do you confirm decay is shrinking?**
Report one weekly Pulse metric, median MQL-to-first-touch time, in leadership reviews, sourced from Zoho with no shadow spreadsheets.
