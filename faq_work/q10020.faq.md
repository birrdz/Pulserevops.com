## FAQ

**How do you automate CAC payback for event-sourced pipeline on Pipedrive without another tool?**
Capture event source and cost on native Pipedrive deal fields, then automate the payback calculation from won-deal value. No separate analytics platform is required to start.

**Which Pipedrive fields prove CAC payback is tracked?**
Define 3-5 proof fields such as lead source = event, event cost, closed-won value, and payback months. Done is clean data on those fields, not activity counts.

**Why is event-sourced pipeline tricky for CAC payback?**
Event costs are lumpy and hit before any deal closes, so payback looks inflated early. Tagging deals to their event in Pipedrive lets the payback math attribute cost correctly.

**Who owns the CAC-payback automation?**
A single RevOps DRI owns the rules and a rollback plan, so payback is calculated one way for every event-sourced deal.

**How do you confirm payback is accurate?**
Report CAC payback months as one weekly Pulse metric in leadership reviews, sourced from Pipedrive with no shadow spreadsheets.
